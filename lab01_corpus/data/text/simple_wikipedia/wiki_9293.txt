Tom Skerritt
Thomas Roy Skerritt (August 25, 1933 – October 21, 2026) was an American actor. He was born in Detroit, Michigan. He was best known for his roles in the films M*A*S*H (1970), Alien (1979), and Top Gun (1986). He won an Emmy Award in 1993 and a Saturn Award in 2011.
Skerritt died from natural causes on October 21, 2026, at his home in Seattle, Washington at the age of 93.
