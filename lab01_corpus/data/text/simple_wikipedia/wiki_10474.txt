Vanilla Ninja
Vanilla Ninja is a music group made up of three women named Lenna Kuurmaa, Piret Järvis and Kerli Kivilaan. Previous members are Katrin Siska, Triinu Kivilaan & Maarja Kivi. They in the Eurovision Song Contest 2005 with the song "Cool Vibes", they finished 8th in the Final. They in the Eurovision Song Contest 2026 with the song "", they performed in the first semi-final which took place on 12 May 2026, they failed to qualify for the final after finishing 11th in the semi-final.
