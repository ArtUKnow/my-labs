Nelvana
Nelvana Limited (; often called Nelvana International or simply Nelvana) is a Canadian animation studio. Founded in July 30, 1971 in Toronto, Ontario, Canada by Michael Hirsh, Patrick Loubert, and Clive A. Smith. The company is now owned by Corus Entertainment. It is named after a local comic book superheroine of the 1940s.
It specializes mainly in the field of child-friendly television series and movies.
