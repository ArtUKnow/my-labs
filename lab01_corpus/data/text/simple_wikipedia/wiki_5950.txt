Hokkaidō Prefecture
is a special Japanese prefecture in Hokkaidō region on the island Hokkaidō. It is the most northern prefecture of Japan.
Sapporo is the capital city of Hokkaido.
2008 (Heisei 20): The 34th G8 summit was held in Toyako.
Geography
Hokkaidō is the 2nd largest island of Japan
Shrines and Temples
Hokkaidō jingū was the chief Shinto shrine (ichinomiya) of Ezo, which was an ancient name for the Hokkaidō region.
