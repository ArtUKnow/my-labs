1000
1000 (M) was a leap year starting on Monday in the Julian calendar. In the proleptic Gregorian calendar, it was an exceptional common year starting on Wednesday. It was the 1000th year of the Common Era (CE) and Anno Domini (AD) designations, the last year of the 1st millennium and 10th century, and the first year of the 1000s decade.
It is one of the only seven years to use just one Roman numeral. The seven are 1 AD (I), 5 AD (V), 10 AD (X), 50 AD (L), 100 AD (C), 500 AD (D), and 1000 AD (M).
