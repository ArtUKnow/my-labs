Carnivore
A carnivore is an animal, or less commonly a plant, which eats only meat. Predators commonly hunt and kill their own prey. Scavengers are carnivores which eat animals they did not kill themselves. Carnivores which eat mainly or only insects are called insectivores. Carnivores which eat mainly or only fish are called piscivores.
The word "carnivore" describes just the scientific order Carnivora. Most Carnivora strictly eat meat. Some are to some degree omnivore s, and a few such as the Giant Panda are vegetarian.

Birds
All birds of prey, such as hawks, eagles, falcons and owls

Reptiles
All crocodilians, such as alligators, crocodiles, gharials and caimans
All snakes, such as cobras, vipers, pythons and boas
Some lizards, such as most skinks and all monitor lizards
Some turtles, including the snapping turtle and most sea turtles
Fish and amphibians
All frogs and toads
Almost all sharks, such as tiger, great white, nurse and reef sharks
Most teleosts, such as tuna, marlin, salmon, and bass

Most crustaceans, such as the coconut crab are mainly omnivorous, but eat some prey and carrion
