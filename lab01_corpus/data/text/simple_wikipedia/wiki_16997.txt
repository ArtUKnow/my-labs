Hatikvah
"Hatikvah" (, , meaning "The Hope") is a Jewish folk song used as the national anthem of Israel. The words of the song are based on a nine-stanza poem written by poet Naftali Herz Imber in 1877. About a decade later, his words were put to music by composer Shmuel Cohen.
