Nigger
Nigger is a slur used against black people. It is often used by racists.
Origin
The word originated as a mispronunciation of the feminine form of the word "negro", which is "negra". In Portuguese, "preto" means the color black and "negro" means black people, but in Spanish, "negro" means both the color black and black people.
History
Before 1865, many black people in the United States were slaves. In this time, "nigger" often meant a slave. Writers like Joseph Conrad, Mark Twain, and Charles Dickens used this word in their books, even in non-racist ways.
