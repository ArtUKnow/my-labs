Connect Four
Connect Four is a simple game. To win, players must put four of the same color markers in the yellow square so that they touch. The yellow player usually starts, dropping a tile down one row, so that it falls to the bottom. Then the red player follows, dropping a red tile in any row. The first player to get 4 of the same color tiles to touch each other in a straight line (Up and down, left and right, or diagonally) wins and the board is cleared. Connect Four is a solved game.
