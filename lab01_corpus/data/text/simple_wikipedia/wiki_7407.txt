John Cleese
John Marwood Cleese (born 27 October 1939) is an English actor, comedian, producer and writer. He was born in Weston-super-Mare, England. He graduated from Downing College, Cambridge. He was a member of the comedy team Monty Python. He starred in the television sitcom Fawlty Towers that he co wrote with his first wife, Connie Booth, who played the role of Polly Sherman on the show. He played the role of Q in the James Bond movies The World Is Not Enough and Die Another Day.
