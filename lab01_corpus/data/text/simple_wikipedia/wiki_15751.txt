of Malmö]]
Malmö is the third largest city in Sweden. In 2017, about 333,633 people lived there. It is in the south west part of Sweden, by the sea. The Öresund bridge goes from Malmö to Copenhagen. In the 15th century, Malmö was one of Denmark's largest cities.
Malmö has the biggest Middle Eastern community in Sweden. It is warm in the summer. In winter, it is not quite as cold as in other parts of Sweden.
