Crawley
Crawley is a town in West Sussex, England, near to Gatwick Airport. Crawley was built into a New Town in 1947.
When planners built Crawley, they wanted to make it easy and safe for families. Instead of building one giant, messy city, they broke it down into smaller pieces called neighbourhoods. Every neighbourhood had its own primary school, some shops, a church, and a pub. You could walk to all of them without crossing busy roads. Crawley now has fourteen neighbourhoods.
Some famous people from Crawley are the musical group The Cure, football player and manager Gareth Southgate, and comedian Romesh Ranganathan.
Neighborhoods
Pound Hill is the largest neighbourhood in Crawley. It is on the east of Crawley. It was the first one to be split into two wards - Pound Hill North and Pound Hill South.
Furnace Green is a neighbourhood located East of the town centre.
The newest neighborhood in Crawley is called Forge Wood.
Three Bridges Station is the biggest and busiest railway station in Crawley.
