Jimmy Swaggart
Jimmy Lee Swaggart (March 15, 1935 – July 1, 2025) was an American Pentecostal televangelist. His church, Family Worship Center, is in Baton Rouge, Louisiana. Swaggart wrote about 50 Christian books. He sold over 15 million records worldwide as a gospel artist. He also received a Grammy Award nomination.
Career
Swaggart first started his television ministry in 1975. The show airs nationally across the U.S. and overseas. The weekly Jimmy Swaggart Telecast and A Study in the Word are shown nationwide and overseas on 78 channels in 104 countries and live over the Internet. He preaches a message called "The Cross" which is that you can only get to heaven through Jesus.
He was a very controversial figure, too. He was caught with a prostitute in 1987, but later asked God (and the public) for forgiveness. He was caught again in 1991.
In 1981, Swaggart received a Grammy Award nomination for Best Gospel Performance, Traditional for his album Worship.
Swaggart held many crusades in Argentina, Canada, Chile, Costa Rica, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Jamaica, Liberia, Nicaragua, Panama, Paraguay, Peru, Russia, and South Africa.
Despite being known as an important figure in the Christian Right, Swaggart was politically independent. However, he was a supporter of President Donald Trump.
Personal life
Swaggart was born in Ferriday, Louisiana. He died in Baton Rouge on July 1, 2025 at the age of 90.
