Toto (band)
Toto (stylized as TOTO) is a rock group that formed in Los Angeles in 1977, that was popular in the early 1980's. Their best known song is "Africa" (1982). Their best known albums are "TOTO", "TOTO IV", and "The Seventh One".
The current band members are Joseph Williams, Steve Lukather and David Paich.
The former band members were David Hungate, Fergie Frederiksen, Mike Porcaro, Jeff Porcaro, Simon Phillips, Greg Phillinganes, Jean-Michel Byron, Bobby Kimball, Keith Carlock and Steve Porcaro.
