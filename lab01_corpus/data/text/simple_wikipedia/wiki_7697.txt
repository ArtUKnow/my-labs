Ball
A ball is a round object (usually spherical, but can sometimes be ovoid) with several uses such as sports. A ball can be either hollow and inflated, such as a basketball, or solid, such as a ball bearing. Ball-like is used metaphorically sometimes to denote something spherical or spheroid.
Different types of balls include:
Sports balls (footballs, soccer balls, baseballs, Tennis balls, etc. [with ball typically being slang as in "play ball"])
Playground balls
Cage Balls
Yoga balls
Bouncy balls
Medicine balls
Hopper balls
Bowling ball
Armadillos and human beings can curl up into a ball, humans sometimes make balls with the fist
The testes (or testis in some parts of the world) are sometimes called balls. One testicle would be a ball (singular).
