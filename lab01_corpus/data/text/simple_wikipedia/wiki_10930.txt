Madonna (disambiguation)
Madonna (Italian for "my lady") can have different meanings. They are isted below. Commonly it is used for the depiction of Mary, and for the singer.
Art, incuding religious art
A depiction of Mary, often with child (Jesus), see Mary, mother of Jesus. Very often this is a statue, sometimes it is also done as a small painting (icon)
Benois Madonna, a painting by Leonardo Da Vinci, completed 1478
Madonna del Prato, a painting by Bellini, completed 1505
Madonna del Prato, a painting by Raphael, completed 1506
A motif by Evard Munch, see Madonna (Munch). He used it for five paintings, done between 1894 and 1897, and a lithograph, in 1895, also see List_of_paintings_by_Edvard_Munch
