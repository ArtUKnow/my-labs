Can a black hole hide another black hole "inside"?
This question is not about a BH colliding with another BH
and not about which coordinate system is the best to
represent a BH geometry
On the earth surface in 3D we see every day
that a mountain can hide another mountain.
A mountain behind a mountain.
In spacetime in 4D, can a black hole hide another black hole i.e. "inside"?
A black hole inside a black hole.
Is it possible for another black hole to appear inside an existing BH. Is it possible to have embedded BH even if we
cannot observe this situation outside of the larger blackhole?
This is rather a logical question which could also be asked as:
is there any rule in GR or computation which demonstrate that
a black hole inside another black hole cannot appear?
