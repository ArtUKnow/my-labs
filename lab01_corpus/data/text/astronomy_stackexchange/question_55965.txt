Entanglement in- and outside the observable universe
Consider the limit of the observable universe.
Assume we received and analysed a photon coming from a distant region close to the edge of the observable universe.
If we believed - based on energy measurement - that the photon we encountered was a result of annihilation of a particle with known mass, Would we not be right in assuming that another photon had entered space outside our observable universe, thereby giving us a measure of knowledge about this unobservable volume of space?
