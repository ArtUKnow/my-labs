Can stable contact binary planets exist
Objects such as asteroids and comets can orbit close enough in binaries that their surfaces overlap and form what a single object. The same process can happen with binary stars. Both of these objects are relatively stable. However I cannot find much on the possibility of contact binary terrestrial or gas giant planets. Are such objects, that consist of two planetary mass objects with overlapping/touching surfaces, possible? And if not, why are contact binary stars possible? What about brown dwarfs if not? The closest I can find to a planetary mass contact binary is the 600 km wide trans-neptunian object 19521 Chaos, which occulted a star and revealed a two-lobed shape, which suggests either a close binary or contact binary object.
