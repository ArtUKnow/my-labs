Why is the terminator line not orange-red in photos from space?
As you know, the sun's rays at dawn and sunset are painted in golden-pink colors, and the objects on which they fall, primarily clouds, snow covers and water surfaces, are also colored. This happens because the red part of the spectrum is better refracted by the earth's atmosphere.
It would be completely logical to expect that earth's terminator line in photographs from space would be colored in the same color.
