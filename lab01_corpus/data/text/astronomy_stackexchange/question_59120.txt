Is there an upper limit to how big a finite universe might be?
If the universe is finite, is there an upper limit to its possible size, such that the universe must either be infinite or smaller than some maximum size? Or any reason why a smaller size would be more likely than a larger one? In other words, even a finite universe would be overwhelmingly likely to be vastly, unimaginably larger than the minimum size implied by the smallest curvature we could (and don't) detect. Right?
