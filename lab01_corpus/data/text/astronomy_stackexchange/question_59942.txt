A good, up-to-date review of all periodicity discovery techniques (folding, etc.)
The excellently written January 2010 AstroBetter Wiki Periodicity Analysis already has some dead or now-unhelpful links, and certainly with the data explosion in the last 15 years and intensified searches for periodicities in that data, it is possible that there's been progress.
Is there a good, one-stop-shopping, up-to-date review of all periodicity discovery techniques for sampled data?

It could be a book, or a presentation or collection of lecture notes. I'm interested in something that covers the breadth of the varieties of methods (with citations) rather than a drill-down of what's used with say a single, specific space telescope's data.
