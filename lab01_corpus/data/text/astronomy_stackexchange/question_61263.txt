Is it possible for a meteor the size of the moon to enter our solar system and crash into the Earth?
I don't think so. First I would wonder where could it have come from. I would think something that large would be attracted to the area of the star explosion that created it. If somehow it got ejected outside of the stardust and drifted into empty space and was drawn by our sun into our solar system I would think it would slowly enter into an orbit around the sun. And if the object had a slow spin I think it wouldn't go straight at the Earth. I think it would get caught up in an orbit around the sun that wouldn't come into contact with the Earth.
