How far apart are the centers of the interacting galaxy pair Whirlpool Galaxy (NGC 5194) and NGC 5195?
Also known as M51a and M51b. Looking for a distance in light years, not in degrees of arc.
According to the info boxes on Wikipedia, the distance to the Whirlpool Galaxy is 23.5 M ly ± 6.95 M ly, and NGC 5195 is 25 M ly (no ± given). Considering the margin of error on the Whirlpool is so large, and completely missing on NGC 5195, and the objects so close together, I did not feel it would be valid to simply subtract the two values as even an extremely rough estimate.
A better thought I had was that given the sizes in light years of each of the galaxies and measurements of their sizes in degrees of arc (both of which seem to be available), perhaps the distance between their centers in degrees of arc (perhaps can be found here, in particular: this image) could be converted to light years to provide a rough estimate? I have some doubt about this method too, because I learned that the sizes were measured in terms of surface brightness, which might not correspond nicely to pixels on an image. Still, I started on it. Examining the image in GIMP I measured a distance of 366.8 pixels, and a measurement of 83 pixels for their 1 arcminute scale. So, the two galactic centers are approximately 4.4 arcminutes apart. Measuring the Whirlpool from the image was a problem, and this is where I got stuck. I see some values here, but I couldn't replicate them from the image.
Perhaps there is a better image, better method, or the distance in light years is already known?
