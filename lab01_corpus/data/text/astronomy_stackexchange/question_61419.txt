Practical strategies for calculating XSPEC errors?
I fit a pretty complex model (some 15 free parameters) using XSPEC to some observation data. Even though the fits themselves are fine, calculating the error on those models is proving to be a huge pain. XSpec's Fit.error() method stalls for hundreds of wall-clock hours (believe me, I've measured) and isn't practical to implement. I switched over to MCMC methods for getting 90% confidence intervals but I'm running into constant failures when trying to work with XSPEC and it's Python version, with constant problems in the initialization of the MCMC chain. Is there a practical way to do this in a reasonable amount of time for an observation that's also scientifically accurate?
