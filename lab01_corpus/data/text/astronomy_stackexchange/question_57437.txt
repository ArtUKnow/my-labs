$L[erg/s]=L[erg/s/Hz]\times(C/\lambda)$
$L[erg/s]=L[erg/s/Hz]\times(C/\lambda^2)d\lambda$

$7.67\times {10}^{8} L_\odot$

$1.1\times {10}^{8} L_\odot$
