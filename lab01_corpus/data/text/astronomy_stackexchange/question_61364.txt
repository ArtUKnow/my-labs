What could kick a comet out of a solar system at 58 km/s?
Wikipedia's 3I/ATLAS gives the object's hyperbolic excess velocity $v_{\infty}$ as 58 km/s with respect to the Sun.
In other words when far from the Sun or other stars in interstellar space, it would be moving at about that speed.
This means that when it left (was "kicked out") of it's solar system's gravitational well it would have had to have been given a "delta-v" of at least that much.
What are the main hypothesized mechanisms that could do that to a comet? Could our solar system do it?
