Did a visiting star reshape the outer solar system?
An article here. visiting star reshapes solar system
says that computer simulations back up the idea that trans Neptunian objects could have elongated and out of plane orbits because a star 100 Au from the Sun altered these orbits when it entered the outer solar system. But if this is correct, are there any anomalies in the Oort Cloud that show the visiting sun was actually present? Has the distribution of matter in one part of the Oort cloud been altered compared to the distribution elsewhere in the cloud?
