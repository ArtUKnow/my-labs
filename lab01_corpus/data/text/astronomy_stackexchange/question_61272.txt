What fraction of the sky are Hubble ultra-deep field and JWST deep field?
The Wikipedia articles for Hubble ultra-deep field and JWST deep field give a field of view of 2.4 arcminutes x 2.4 arcminutes.
Hubble ultra-deep field:
the rectangular image is 2.4 arcminutes to an edge, or 3.4
arcminutes diagonally
JWST deep field:
covering an area of sky with an angular size of 2.4 arcminutes
With a lot of search, I confirmed that it matches with the primary sources:
Hubble:
Dimensions This image is roughly 2.4 arcminutes wide.
JWST:
Dimensions Image is about 2.4 arcmin across
Now, the entire sky is $360*60 = 21600$ $arcminutes^2$.
Therefore, the fraction of sky covered by the deep fields should be: $(2.4/21600)^2 = 1/81000000$.
However, the same Hubble ultra-deep field wikipedia article goes on to mention:
equal to roughly one twenty-six-millionth of the total area of the sky
Is this a mistake? What are the correct values?
