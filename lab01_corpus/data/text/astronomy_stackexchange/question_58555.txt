Is it possible that we are part of a relatively small object adjacent to a very large one?
I wonder hypothetically if it could be that we are (for example) part of a dust cloud blowing down the street in some huge town. We know that in the observable universe there are objects that are very large and very small (from our perspective). Can we know that beyond the observable universe, the scale of objects remains about the same? That it can't blow up in scale immensely at some point?
