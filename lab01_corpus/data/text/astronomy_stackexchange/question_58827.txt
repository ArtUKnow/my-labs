Confused about what an orbit means now
The other day, my girlfriend who is in her college's astronomy club told me a discussion was sparked in a meeting regarding what orbits really mean. She said someone tried to make an "extreme" argument, something like: "so if an orbit just means you're going around something, the moon orbits the sun!". She didn't agree, because intuitively we don't think the moon orbits the sun; the moon orbits the Earth, which itself orbits the sun, and it goes in a hierarchy... right?
I'm more mathematically inclined than she is, so this made a sour taste in my mouth, and it got me thinking. Why? Why doesn't the moon orbit the sun? The path it takes is certainly not elliptic, but why doesn't it classify as an orbit? Why isn't the sum of orbits an orbit itself? Couldn't you go all out Fourier-mode and make an orbit that follows any shape you want?
