Why does Cepheid Brightness change?
I'm looking for an explanation of the cepheid mechanism. I looked all over the web but, strangely, I found nothing on it (almost nothing).
For the moment, here is what I got :
The theory progressed over the last century, from a binary star system (2 suns rotating around the same gravity point) to a specific matter composition...
The pulsation rests on the Helium ionization (the transparent He2 gas will turn into He3, which is an opaque gas). Step by step we can resume here :
1] The Helium layer is condensed by the gravitation force. Here the temperature increases but, overall, it's the density that increases. This transforms the He2 to He3 (the star becomes opaque)
2] According to Kramer's opacity laws, the opacity is proportional to the density on the temperature. As the density increased quicker than the temperature, the new He3 gaz became opaque.
3] The opaque gas keeps the heat to itself and it takes more and more place
4] This expansion of matter cools down the gas and the He3 turns into He2.
5] The light goes how and we cycle begins again.
The same explanation in a few words is :
Concentration of He2 + Temperature = He3 + Opacity
Opacity = Keep the heat
Heater = Take more space
Take more space/Expansion = Cooling down
Cooling down + He3 = He2 + Transparent layer
And it starts again...
