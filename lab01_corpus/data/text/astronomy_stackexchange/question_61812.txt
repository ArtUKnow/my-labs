How to tell if two locations have the same longtitude with naked human eyes
As a follow-up to this question, I was wondering about another scenario. Suppose there is a person who has been living for a few years in Mexico at -103 longitude. Then he migrated to Canada and has lived there for several years now, but at the same longitude. Could he figure it out without the help of any compass, clock, or any other modern aid, whether both the places he lived in were on the same longitude? He can only gaze at the night sky and track the sun's motions. He has keenly observed their cycles for at least one full year in both places. Assume that the person has a very good memorization ability but no access to the internet or phone-a-friend.
