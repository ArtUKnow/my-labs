Why are my calculations for the Moon’s transit time allways incorrect?
I am following the article stjarnhimlen's Computing the Moon's rise/set times tutorial to calculate the moonrise and moonset times.
At first, I was trying to calculate the moon transit time, but there was always a difference of up to 40 minutes compared to my tests on MoonCalc site, and I don’t know the reason.
Example of Moon Transit Calculation:
Date: 2 October 2025 00:00 UTC+3
My Result: 2 October 2025 19:21:10 UTC+3
MoonCalc's Result: 2 October 2025 20:05:55 UTC+3
Moon transit Equation:
$$
\text{GMST}_0 = L_{\odot} + 180^\circ
$$
$$
\text{UTC}_{\text{Lunar Transit}} =
\frac{\alpha_{\text{topo}} - \text{GMST}_0 - \lambda}{15}
$$
$$
\text{UTC}_{\text{Lunar Transit}} =
\left( \left( \text{UTC}_{\text{Lunar Transit}} \bmod 24 \right) + 24 \right) \bmod 24
$$
where:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{GMST}_0 &: \text{ Greenwich mean sidereal time at 0h UT} \\
L_{\odot} &: \text{ solar mean longitude (in degrees)} \\
\alpha_{\text{topo}} &: \text{ topocentric right ascension (in degrees)} \\
\lambda &: \text{ observer's geographic longitude (in degrees, east positive)}
\end{aligned}
$$
