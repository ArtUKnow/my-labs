Would an astronaut's eye (either in orbit or on the surface of a moon orbiting one of these planets) see the same amount of brightness/detail that is present in the photos?

Or are the outer planets too dark for our human eyes to see them as brilliantly - just as it becomes hard to see things in the dark?
