Why does Jupiter spin so fast but not the Sun?
The mainstream interpretation is that when Jupiter was being formed from a giant disk of gas and dust, the conservation of angular momentum dictated that it spun faster as it grew smaller.
At the same time the Sun rotates at a "moderately" 1~2 miles per second which is nowhere close to Jupiter's rotational speed. Obviously if you were to form a star your "initial disk" just had to be much, much larger than what you would a gas giant.
So, what happened to the sun? Was it because of its interaction with all the planets?
