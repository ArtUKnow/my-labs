Are solar systems formed from a gas centrifuge?
The solar system is said to have formed from the remnants of earlier stars and their contents exploding into space.
The elements of these stars are formed by nucleosynthesis.
These exploding stars would have created an interstellar gas medium in which the contents of these stars mixed.
With the concentration of these gases they would have begun to move under their own gravity a motion that is reflected in the solar system and galaxies we observe today.
Once this motion had begun would the gases that made up this medium form a gas centrifuge to separate out the individual elements of this mixture.
Would this centrifuge be the reason of the formation of the planets compositions and their proximity to the sun aswell as the proposed formation of the oort cloud that could have been the source of water on earth?
