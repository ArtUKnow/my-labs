Collect raw GNSS log using GnssLogger Android application.

Analyze the log using gnss_lib_py (the problem is that it needs the ephemeris files and these files take at least a day to be uploaded, so I cannot calculate latitude and longitude in the same day or real-time).
I installed and used the GNSS compare Android application (the latitude and longitude provided by this app are exactly the same for GPS and Galileo, shouldn't we expect some difference?).
I have installed RTKGPS+ android application (it is somewhat complex and I am not sure if I am using it correctly and it keeps crashing).
and some other tools...
I still have not found a reliable way to calculate latitude and longitude of an Android device in real-time or at least in the same day.
My question:
