I saw three pulsing lights moving across the sky
I live in Southern Maine and two nights ago I was outside looking at the sky while taking my dog to do his business. I noticed what I thought was a satellite moving across the sky from east to west. I see plenty of satellites out here. But then I noticed it was incrementally increasing and dimming in brightness--2 seconds grows bright, 2 seconds goes dim. It wasn't a flashing light like a plane, it was pulsing. It did this for about a minute and then vanished. There were no clouds in the sky at this point, though they did roll in later.
A few seconds after it vanished another one appeared going west to east. This one lasted maybe 15-20 seconds, and it was following the same 2 second pulsing pattern. Then 10 minutes later I saw another one for about 10 seconds going from southeast to west.
So what are these?
