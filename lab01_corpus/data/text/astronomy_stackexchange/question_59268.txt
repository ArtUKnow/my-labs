Does WGS 84 use a flat metric?
The Geocentric Celestial Reference System (GCRS) and the International Terrestrial Reference System (ITRS) are based on an approximate Schwarzschild metric, as discussed for instance in Soffel & al. 2003 or in Kaplan 2005.
