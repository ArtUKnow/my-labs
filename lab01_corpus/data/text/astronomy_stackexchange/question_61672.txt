Earth Shadow's Enlargement Refinement
I even read in Explanatory Supplement of Astronomical Almanac if umbra enlargement factor was 1.02 (2% increased) for $\pi_{moon}$, $s_{sun}$, and $\pi_{sun}$ terms. The equation becomes: $r_u = 1.02(\pi_{moon} - s_{sun} + \pi_{sun})$
as we know that 1.02 is factor due to atmospheric refraction
However, Danjon (Analysis os lunar crater timings, 1842-2011) considered to multiply enlargement factor into $\pi_{moon}$ term only. The equation becomes: $r_u = (0.998340 + h) π_{moon} − s_{sun} + π_{sun}$
which h is notional eclipse-forming layer (NEL) height, equals 75 km or 0.0118 Earth Radii (or in the paper derived into 87 km or 0.0136 Earth Radii)
while Chauvenet still multiply with 1.02 after Danjon's equation, so the equation becomes: $r_u = 1.02((0.998340 + h) π_{moon} − s_{sun} + π_{sun})$
Thus, is it relevant if we still use 1.02 factor when lunar horizontal parallax was already multiplied?
