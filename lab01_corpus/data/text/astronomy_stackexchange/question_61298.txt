$R_a$ = rate per day that telescope A detects a particular transient based on total number of detections per year (rate published by the telescope)
$L_b$ = number of days < 1yr that telescope B was searching for a different type of transient (assuming $L_a$ perfectly covers the same period and more)
$R_E$ = rate per day we'll use for the analysis = $\frac{R_a \times L_b}{ 365}$
