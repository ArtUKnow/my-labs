What is the farthest distance from which auroras on Earth have been observed by humans?
We've seen many photos of auroras on Earth (aurora borealis and australis) taken from the International Space Station, which orbits around 400 km above Earth.
This made me wonder — what is the farthest distance from which an aurora has been observed directly by a human (either visually or via photographs taken by astronauts)?
For example, did any Apollo astronauts report seeing auroras from lunar orbit or during their journey to the Moon (~384,000 km from Earth)? Have any other deep space missions with human crews captured them?
I'm mainly interested in confirmed human observations or astronaut photography, not satellite or robotic instruments.
