Why is Epsilon Indi Ab 2° Celsius?
I was reading the Wikipedia page for the exoplanet Epsilon Indi Ab.
It orbits around its parent star every 173.6 years in a distance of 28.4 AU as provided in the first infobox of the article.
The parent star is in fact a star system consisting of three stars called Epsilon Indi. The stars are Epsilon Indi A, Ba, and Bb respectively.
Epsilon Indi A, the star that the mentioned exoplanet revolves around has a surface temperature of only 4649 K which makes it even colder than our Sun.
My question: How does an exoplanet orbiting around a cold star at such a huge distance have a temperature of near 275 K?
