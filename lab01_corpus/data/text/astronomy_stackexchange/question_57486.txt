Rectification of M-type spectra
I would appreciate references and/or tips on estimating the continuum
in the spectra of M dwarf stars. The ultimate aim is to obtain radial
velocities from sequences of spectra by comparing to a template spectrum.
The continuum shapes of the template spectrum and those of the stars
of interest may differ, hence the necessity to rectify the spectra.
The specific wavelength range available is 5100-6100 Angstrom.
