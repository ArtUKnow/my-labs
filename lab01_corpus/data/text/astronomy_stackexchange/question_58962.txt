What would happen to a mile-wide sphere of water ice that slammed into the Earth at 90 degrees straight down, at 35,000 MPH?
Although the question sounds silly to me, my friend says it's a hot question on Quora, so I told him I'd ask what the smart people think.
It seems various factions say it will:
Melt and rain
Burst into a fine mist
Hit the ground and shatter
Explode in the air like a bolide
Have the same effect as a rock
Whatever idle minds can come up with
Other than the fact that It would have about five seconds between hitting the atmosphere and the ground, and that the ice would be super hard after being near absolute zero, the only thing I can say is that I guess it would smash into the earth just like any other asteroid. The damage is done by the momentum of the mass, and that can't change, whether it's water or solid.
The phase don't enter into it.
