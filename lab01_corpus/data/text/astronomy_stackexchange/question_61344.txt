Converting heliocentric galactic coordinates to ICRS using astropy
I have the "heliocentric galactic" cartesian coordinates of Proxima Centauri as given in the paper Kervella et al 2017 (Table B.1 of https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.03495). I would like to convert these coordinates to ICRS using astropy.
The position and velocity are given in heliocentric cartesian coordinates (X,Y,Z) with velocity (U,V,W). Quoting the authors of the paper, "The heliocentric Galactic coordinates of the barycenter of α Cen and of Proxima are presented in Table B.1, together with their heliocentric space velocity... We followed the classical convention of X and U increasing toward the Galactic center, Y and V positive toward the Galactic direction of rotation, and Z and W positive toward the North Galactic pole."
I've tried creating a SkyCoord object as follows:
proxima = SkyCoord(
representation_type = 'cartesian',
x = 0.90223 * u.pc,
y = -0.93599 * u.pc,
z = -0.04386 * u.pc,
frame = 'galactic',
u = -29.390 * u.km/u.s,
v = 1.883 * u.km/u.s,
w = 13.777 * u.km/u.s
)
I get an error that keywords 'x', 'y', and 'z' are not recognized, even though I specified the representation_type 'cartesian'. Perhaps because I'm using a galactic frame it is expecting latitude and longitude?
Once I can create a SkyCoord object with these galactic cartesian coordinates, I can convert it to ICRS cartesian using "proxima.icrs.cartesian". But how can I create a SkyCoord object with heliocentric galactic cartesian coordinates?
Thank you for any help!
