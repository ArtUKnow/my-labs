As a result, we have the phenomenon called lunar parallax, which influences on observable Moon's declination in the sky and surely impacts the lunar phase angle.
Let's imagine an example:
The full Moon during a major lunar standstill: the phase angle will be at most 175 degrees (the difference between the ecliptic and lunar orbit is about 5 degrees), although this Moon's phase angle for the latitude of 70 degrees north will be different than for the latitude of 70 degrees south.
