What is left behind when a Type 1A supernova occurs?
When low or intermediate mass stars die, they collapse into white dwarfs. When high mass stars die, they go supernova and their cores collapse into neutron stars or black holes.
However, if a white dwarf accrues enough mass by pulling mass off of another star beyond a critical mass, a runaway thermonuclear fusion event will cause the white dwarf to explode, forming a type 1A supernova.
