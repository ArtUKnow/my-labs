Why the ancient Greeks associated planets with gods?
They associated the Sun with Helios and the Moon with Selene, with the names of these gods also being used for these astronomical bodies. For other astronomical bodies, a name and an associated god were present: Phainon (Saturn) was associated with Cronus, Phaethon (Jupiter) with Zeus, Pyroeis (Mars) with Ares, Phosporos (Venus) with Aphrodite, and Stilbon (Mercury) with Hermes. (And today these planets are named after the Roman equivalents of these gods).
