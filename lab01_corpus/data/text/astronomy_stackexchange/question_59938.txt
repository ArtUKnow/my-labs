Why do Neptune, Jupiter, Saturn and Uranus spin faster than the Earth?
Why do Neptune, Jupiter, Saturn and Uranus spin faster than the Earth? They are much further out than the Earth from the Sun, and they are far more massive. Jupiter has >300 times the mass of Earth and its angular rotation rate is more than twice as fast!
The nebular hypothesis and all its variants do not give a clear answer. They just say they, "sucked up more mass and angular momentum" therefore they spin faster during their formation. How?
When I spin a basketball or a book on my finger, I am imparting angular momentum using my hand. What is the "hand" in this example? Where did their axial angular momentum come from exactly? Their axial angular momentum is conserved, where did it come from?
