Whereas an anti-tail had been seen for solar system comets as a
temporary perspective effect when the Earth crossed the comets’
orbital plane, this is clearly not the case with 3I/ATLAS. The
anti-tail was apparent in the first Hubble Space Telescope image,
taken on July 21, 2025, when 3I/ATLAS was approaching the Sun from a

Hubble image taken on November 30, 2025, when 3I/ATLAS was receding
away from the Sun at a distance of 1.91 AU from Earth — as reported
here. The anti-tail was also apparent in thousands of images taken in
between these dates.
