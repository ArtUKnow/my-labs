What difference does it make to a Galaxy if the central black hole spins at an angle to the galactic plane?
There is an interesting article here about Sagittarius A*, the black hole at the centre of the Milky Way:
Sagittarius A
It has an accretion disc that is inclined to the galactic plane and may have formed 9 billion years ago. What difference would there be to the rest of the Milky Way if this accretion disc was in the galactic plane? Would the distribution of stars be very different? Would Earth still move above and below the galactic plane as it orbits the galactic centre?
