If Pluto never comes close enough to the sun to melt the nitrogen ice, why does sublimation occurs?
Based on this answer, it is concluded that Pluto never receives sufficient amount of sunlight to melt nitrogen ice. Yet, sublimation pits is observed across Sputnik Planitia. From here:
These are likely to be sublimation pits, depressions in the surface when ice turned directly into a gas from sunlight. They probably start very small and grow over time as the Sun shines down on them.
