Did Earth affect the evolution of the Solar System?
We all know that Jupiter played a great role in the Solar System by cleaning up debris and dust from the Solar System, and deflecting asteroids. Saturn also played a similar role. Uranus and Neptune helped Earth by sending water ice-rich comets our way to enrich our planet with water, and now deflect rowdy Kuiper Belt objects.
Earth too has its own specialities, aside from being the only planet with life. For starters, Earth and its moon have the largest mass ratio (aside from Pluto and Charon). There is no other moon we have discovered yet that is comparably large compared to its parent planet. Also, it happens to be the largest terrestrial planet in the Solar System.
Out of curiosity, I think that Earth did play a role in the evolution of our solar system. Since it is the largest rocky planet in our solar system, it must have had exerted some gravitational influence in the inner Solar System. Of course I know it cleared out its planetary neighborhood during its formation. I'm just curious as to whether Earth's presence had some effect on the evolution of our solar system.
