Compressed helical spring powering an early stage of rocket
We take a (ball point pen ink tube) and (press release/retract spring) as a model for storing potential energy (PE). The ink tube can be projected up by the spring when kept free.
Is it possible to design a humongous but lightweight helical spring storing torsional PE and to deliver controlled release thrust in order to eliminate first/early stage of a launch vehicle? Some sort of a helical slingshot? The spring is to be made with carbon or Kevlar material so it has stored energy per unit mass in an earth bound experiment.
Preliminary energy calculations and dynamics indicate a feasibility.
Energy saving would be considerable as compression spring is easily loaded by power screws driven by electric motors.
