Telescope in a crater? How different are the bowl shapes?
When building large stationary single-dish radio telescopes, one of the constraints to location is that the landscape should already have approximately the right shape, cutting down on construction costs.
When looking at geologically inactive bodies (say, the Moon), an obvious feature of the landscape is all the craters. They look "bowl shaped". Radio telescopes are also "bowl shaped".
It's evident that such craters are a good starting point for building a telescope dish, but how good? In general: How much does the geometry of an impact crater differ from a telescope?
