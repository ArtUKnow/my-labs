After almost 40 years of research on space stations in LEO, what is there we still didn't find out?
The first modular space station Mir was launched in early 1986 into orbit, so the time of research on the Mir and the ISS (and the Tiangongs) combined is now more than 38 years. If you include Salyut 6 and 7, it's more than 46 years of LEO research. After all those years I'm curious what there still is we didn't find out on a LEO space station.
One example coming into my mind is that we could let someone spend a new duration record on the ISS, e.g. two years, as a preparation for a possible Mars mission. But is a new duration mission thought about? Other than that, what else is there we still might not have found out after decades of crew exchanges on LEO space stations? And if they're only for the purpose of Earth observation there: can crews on space stations do something Earth observation satellites can't?
