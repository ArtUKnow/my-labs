The rocket's close to 30 deg relative to the local horizon, down from the 90 deg it had at launch.

My impression from videos I've watched is that it's opposite the direction of the gravity turn, but the "angle relative to surface" plot on this flightclub.io simulation seems to suggest it's along the direction of the gravity turn...
Does anyone know for sure?
