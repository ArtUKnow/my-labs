The paywalled article discusses the QB50 project, which used differential drag.
But the last paragraph of the block quote from the paywalled article seems to cite Pini Gurfil of Technion University saying that solar panels of cubesats can use solar photon pressure to actually raise altitude!
Orient a satellite so that its solar panels are hit by the maximum possible amount of light in the part of its orbit when it is receding from the sun, and the minimum amount when it is approaching it, and the spacecraft will gain speed, and therefore altitude. For a CubeSat smaller than a shoebox, with solar panels the size of two old-fashioned record-album sleeves, harnessing sunlight in this way should lift its orbit by several dozen metres a day, according to Dr Gurfil.
