Did I really see that much detail on the ISS, or something else?
The evening of Jan.3, 2024, while dragging bins uphill to our garage near Olympia, WA, clouds suddenly cleared to reveal a bright sliver moon, &…...the ISS?!? I was facing nominally south.
It was clearly 2 large, goldish-silver wing arrays on top, with a darker body just barely visible, connecting to what I guessed were the lower arrays. Like a stylistic, tech-dragonfly with extras on its tail.
The thing appeared to be about half the size of the sliver moon, due to its position relative to the moon’s.
It seemed to stand still, as-if posing for a photo. Then, clouds quickly hid both.
I’d never seen it before. But, this seemed to be it. I just cannot grok how could I see its complex shape with naked eyes, when others report only seeing a “bright spot”. My vision is not that acute..?
What I saw, did resemble models of the ISS.
Is there ANYthing else that could look like an ISS model, that would have been in-line with the sliver moon, & just as bright, seeming on the published ISS flight path??
