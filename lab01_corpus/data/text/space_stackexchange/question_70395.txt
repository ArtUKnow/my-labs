Why was the choice made to not put beacon lights on satellites that can be seen from low earth orbit?
A lot of people question space. A fair question to astronauts and the fine people at NASA is:
Why has no one ever attached a bright shining beacon to a satellite in low earth orbit? They could put a bright flashing light on something in space and say “look, there it is, it's really up there!”. Why haven't they done that? Are the lights really not bright enough?
