2020 SO was captured by Earth's gravity on Nov. 8, 2020. It will
escape in March 2021
This very special orbit could not be calculated as a two body problem.
At least we need to calculate the influence of the Sun and the Earth.
With the analysis of more than 170 detailed measurements of 2020 SO's
position over the last three months, including observations made by
the NASA-funded Catalina Sky Survey in Arizona and ESA's (European
Space Agency's) Optical Ground Station in Tenerife, Spain, the impact
of solar radiation pressure became evident and confirmed 2020 SO's
low-density nature. The next step was to figure out where the
suspected rocket booster could have come from.
So a three body problem is not enough, the solar radiation pressure should be calculated too.
