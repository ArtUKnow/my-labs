Could the 2029 approach of 99942 Apophis perturb the orbits of GSO satellites?
Thinking about this question, I remembered that on the 13th of April 2029, the asteroid 99422 Apophis will pass within geostationary orbit distance - Apophis will be around 31,600 kilometres at closest approach, GSO distance is 35,786 km.
Wikimedia Commons image of the path of closest approach
The mass of Apophis is 'only' around 6.1×1010 kg and as can be seem from the image above, probably won't directly pass through the orbits of any satellites.
So if a GSO satellite is close enough to Apophis during this approach, could the satellite's orbit be perturbed?
