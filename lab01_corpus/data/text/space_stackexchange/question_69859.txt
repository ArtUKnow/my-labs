Vision on foreign bodies
I think the most evocative data from space exploration is the images and videos from the landings on foreign planets and moons, like Venera on Venus, the Mars rovers and Huygens on Titan. They make me imagine that I stand there on these places in person and look around me at the surroundings.
But... How well would I be able to see with an unaided eye in those places? Venus is close to the Sun but it has a very thick atmosphere that probably blocks much light, Mars is farther from the Sun than Earth and has less than half the sun light, and Titan is both really far from the Sun and also has a thick atmosphere.
How bright would an unaided eye perceive those places to be? Would it be too dark to see anything at all? Assume that I stand at each of those places at the equator at noon. How well would I be able to see?
