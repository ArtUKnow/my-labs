Will Ataxite be the type of nickel-iron sought for asteroid mining?
The high value metals in meteorites and asteroids - mostly Platinum Group Metals - appear associated with the nickel content (The Occurrence of Platinum in Meteorites, F. G. Hawley,1939), ie the highest PGM content is found within the high nickel content meteorites. The meteorite mineral Taenite has the highest nickel content - 20% - 65%. But does Taenite occur on it's own, as a distinct material? Or is it only found as crystals and bands within lower nickel content materials?
Ataxite is a high Taenite content form of meteorite nickel-iron, with a nickel content between about 15 - 30%. If Taenite does not appear on it's own will Ataxite be the preferred type of asteroid metal for mining potential?
