Tradeoffs between mining asteroids vs the Moon
At the moment, it seems like popular sentiment is saying Lunar Mining is significantly better than Near-Earth Asteroid mining. This seems illogical to me:
Near-Earth Asteroids have near zero gravity, but you can use spin gravity to create healthy gravities. The moon has 1/6th gravity, likely in the range where it's bad for bones and muscles.
The moon has enough mass that resources will have been pulled towards the center, and enough mass to compact the surface. A Near-Earth Asteroid is too small for this to be the case.
Getting resources off the moon is easier than the earth, but still harder than off a Near-Earth Asteroid once mined.
My question is, for space infrastructure purposes, what are the tradeoffs between the two?
