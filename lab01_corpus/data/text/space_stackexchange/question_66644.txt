What would have happened if the 2nd L.C.G. bag was torn during the Apollo 13 mission?
In the Apollo 13 movie, when the astronauts build the filter in order to connect the CSM and LM canisters, one of the components needed for that is an L.C.G. bag. One astronaut accidentally rips it. Luckily they have another bag onboard. I wonder what would happen if the other bag was torn too. Could there have been another way to build the filter? Could the astronauts have tried something else to escape the toxic CO2 levels (other than putting on spacesuits)?
Fred Haise figured that the CO2 would be harmless for two people in the LM. If the astronauts really couldn't do anything more, would one of them (need/want to) sacrifice himself so that the other two make it to Earth? And of course, I also wonder if things really went as depicted in the movie, concerning the filtering of the carbon dioxide.
