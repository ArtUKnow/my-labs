What heritage (Space Shuttle era) equipment was to be used to "verify the flight termination system" during the Artemis II launch?
At around T-1:54 hours of the Artemis II launch, it was mentioned that there was a possible issue with the flight termination system (FTS). Then the broadcast commenter mentioned that "Range" requested to retrieve a heritage piece of hardware (Space Shuttle era) from the Vehicle Assembly Building (VAB) to help verify the correct functioning of the FTS. As it was explained, this piece of hardware was able to receive FTS commands.
Transcript of the NASA live stream:
The launch team has been made aware of an issue that the Range is working [...] related to the flight termination system. [...] And so as they continue to work that issue, they've asked for some assistance from the launch team to help verify some of the work they're doing on their FTS system. [...] And in order to do that, the FTS console operator inside the firing room has been asked [...] to go get a piece of heritage equipment inside the VAB that was used during the Space Shuttle Program. And this equipment is able to receive commands by the Range through the FTS.
What was this piece of hardware and how could* it be used to "verify the FTS functioning" without actually triggering bad things on the actual SLS/Orion stack?
*) Later in the coverage (around T-1:16) it was mentioned that the issue had been resolved or worked-around, presumable without using the heritage equipment mentioned earlier.
