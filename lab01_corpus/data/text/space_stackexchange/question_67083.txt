Why are some orbits circular and others elliptical
My understanding is: Due to the inverse square law, the farther away an object is from the sun, the less gravitational pull it has on it. Depending on what its velocity is, under the influence of the sun’s gravity and the law of conservation of angular momentum, its orbit will end up being either an ellipse or a circle (special case of ellipse).
