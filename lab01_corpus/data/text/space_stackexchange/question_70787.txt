How are the Apoapsis and Periapsis calculated for a gaseous planet?
Apogee and Perigee of the earth are calculated on the basis of the distance of the satellite from the sea level of the earth.
This can only be done in celestial bodies where there is a specific solid ground separating it from atmosphere (like Earth or Mars) or vacuum (like the moons of Earth or Mars).
