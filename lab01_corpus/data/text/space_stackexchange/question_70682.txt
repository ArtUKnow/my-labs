What do the colors on NASA nametags mean?
Here is a great picture of the crews of the first and last Shuttle missions together. From left to right: John Young, STS-1 commander; Robert Crippen, STS-1 pilot; Chris Ferguson, STS-135 commander; Doug Hurley, STS-135 pilot; Sandy Magnus, STS-135 mission specialist; and Rex Walheim, STS-135 mission specialist.
Notice the nametags on each astronaut. Some nametags have gold lettering and wings, and others are silver. Most nametags have a blue background, but one is red. What is the significance of the nametag colors?
