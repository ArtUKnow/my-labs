The following is the maths I did to find the predicted values necessary for this to work in order to compare when all is said and done:
Earth:
$$v_1^e=\sqrt{2\mu_s}\sqrt{\frac{r_2}{r_1(r_1+r_2)}}=22.29km/s$$
$$\Delta v_1^e=\sqrt{\frac{\mu_s}{r_1}}\sqrt{\frac{2r_2}{r_1+r_2}}-1=-7.532km/s$$
Mercury:
$$v_2^m=\sqrt{2\mu_s}\sqrt{\frac{r_1}{r_2(r_1+r_2)}}=57.49km/s$$
$$\Delta v_2^m=\sqrt{\frac{\mu_s}{r_2}}\sqrt{1-\frac{2r_1}{r_1+r_2}}=-9.61km/s$$
SMA of transfer:
$$a_h=\frac{r_1+r_2}{2}=0.69355AU$$
Time taken to transfer orbits:
$$t_h=\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{\frac{4\pi^2*a+h^3}{\mu_s}}=119.167 days$$
