What is the Delta V from the Moon to Earth Sun L1?
There are various proposals to build infrastructure at Earth/Sun L1 point, such as discussed in this question. From LEO the Delta V cost to L1 is about 3.5 kms or about kg of propellant in LEO for every kg needed at L1, doubling needed payload to LEO on a project where final mass is already in millions of tonnes.
For this reason a number of proposals have presumed getting most of the structural mass from the moon.
The naive assumption using that answer and charts used here would put the Delta V cost of Moon surface to L1 at around 3kms, similar to LEO. In theory it should be possibly to gravity assist from the Earth to lower that a bit, and also use a fixed launcher on the surface to reduce the needed fuel, but even combining those two there would need to be thrust to take station at L1.
