Using rocket engines as vacuum pumps for high altitude hot fire testing
The test of the rocket's second-stage engine can be carried out using a small nozzle model. Or you can run such test in a dedicated vacuum facility, which can better reflect the performance of the rocket. There are few facilities that can perform such tests, such as NASA's In-Space Propulsion Facility (ISP). These facilities use multi-stage steam ejectors to generate the required vacuum.
We know that steam ejectors use high-speed steam to drive gas to generate vacuum, so why not use rocket engine ejection to generate vacuum directly, which can greatly reduce the development cost of the facility.
