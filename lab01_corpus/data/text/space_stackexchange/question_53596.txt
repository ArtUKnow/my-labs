Small probes to Alpha Centauri powered by light sails with a journey time of 20 years
This news item states that scientists have potentially devised a way to send a number of small probes to Alpha Centauri at quarter the speed of light. The journey would take 20 years and each small probe would have a light sail that would be struck with the light from high energy lasers.
Scientists estimate roughly 100 million individual lasers will be needed to generate the required optical power of about 100GW.
