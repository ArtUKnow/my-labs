Have metamaterials been utilized in space exploration?
Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metamaterial :
A metamaterial … is a type of material engineered to have a property,
typically rarely observed in naturally occurring materials, that is
derived not from the properties of the base materials but from their
newly designed structures. ... usually arranged in repeating patterns,
at scales that are smaller than the wavelengths of the phenomena they
influence.
Those that exhibit a negative index of refraction for particular
wavelengths have been the focus of a large amount of research.
Potential applications of metamaterials are diverse and include ..
super-lenses. Such a lens can allow imaging below the diffraction
limit that is the minimum resolution … that can be achieved by
conventional lenses
Meta materials have been proposed for remote sensing applications measurements of tiny airborne particles
and 6G antennae https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666950123001207#:~:text=In%20fact%2C%20Metamaterial%20antennas%20are,et%20al.%2C%202022)
