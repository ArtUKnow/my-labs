Why does this "satellite antenna at the Laguna Peak Tracking and Injection Facility" look so weird? (two dishes back-to-back)
The video 1967 Navy Satellite Navigation training film (IBM 7094 data center) after 11:57 (older version here) the narrator says:
For every pound of satellite in orbit, there are tons of equipment on Earth that make navigation by satellite possible. Let's look at the ground system.
Satellite tracking stations are located in Maine, Minnesota, Hawaii, and California.
Located here at point Mugu California, the (US) Navy Astronautics Group operates the navigation satellite system. The headquarters building contains the operations, communications and computer centers.
Overlooking Point Mugu is one of the system's two injection stations, the other one is in Minnesota.
Here's some screenshots including a closeup of the disk. Behind what looks like a normal dish reflector is what looks to me like another dish of the same size and shape facing backwards, as if they are in a back-to-back configuration.
