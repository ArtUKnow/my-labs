Is there wood on this Soviet Venus probe, shown in an archival photo?
The BBC's May 10, 2025 Soviet-era spacecraft 'likely' to have re-entered Earth's atmosphere there is a file photo of what is reportedly some Soviet probe bound for Venus but not Kosmos 482.
The lower "hemisphere" (in the orientation shown) is much darker that the "equator" and could be some kind of reentry ablative heat shield.
The Soviet Union launched a number of missions to explore Venus – this probe was from an earlier flight (Getty Images)
However the "equator" of the spherical craft appears to have a very nice wood grain pattern around it. Is it just me, or does that look like (as a wild guess) ash?
