CZ-4B Grid fins
In nov 2019 and sept 2020 the CNSA launched CZ-4B Y38 and CZ-4B Y41. Based on different images I noticed that this Long March 4B had grid fins. On CZ-4B Y41 the grid fins from Y38 were reused making it the first reused component in the Chinese space program
What is the CNSA trying here? are they trying to test components for a future reusable launcher? or do they serve another purpose?
