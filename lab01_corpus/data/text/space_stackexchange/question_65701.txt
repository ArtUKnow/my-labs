Why is NASA waiting until Artemis V to bring a rover?
Crewed moon rovers will be a 55 year old technology by the time Artemis III and IV launch, and historically they greatly increased the amount of time lunar astronauts could spend doing science instead of walking between points of interest, so why is NASA waiting until V to bring even an unpressurized rover? Whats more, V seems to be a specifically bad choice, as the rover is planned to be launched alongside Orion in the SLS stack, because they are using the Blue Origin lander for that mission; if they sent the rover on III or IV, they could easily fit the rover, or even a pressurized rover, in the larger Starship lander.
