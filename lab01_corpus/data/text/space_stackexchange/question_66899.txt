What would happen today if a spacecraft failed in orbit with astronauts inside?
Some spaceship - like a Dragon, a Soyuz or a Starliner - leaves the ISS and is doing the orbital maneuvers needed to land where it wants to. And, at this point... they have a problem. The spacecraft propulsion system stops working and there is no way to fix it by remote commands. It remains on the orbit where the failure occurred.
What would happen?
As far as I know the only way to save the astronauts is if another spaceship - either from the ISS or from the Earth - is launched, docks to the stranded spaceship, gets the astronauts out and then it comes back.
Is there any plan for this type of failure? Imagine the nightmare listening to the astronauts as their air supply slowly runs out.
