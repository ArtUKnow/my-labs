From the 50th Lunar and Planetary Science Conference 2019 (LPI Contrib. No. 2132) The Calibration Target for the Mars 2020 SHERLOC Instrument (archived):
Three targets additionally serve unique education and public outreach purposes. The Mars meteorite, a slice of Sayh al Uhaymir 008 (SaU008) collected in Oman in 1999, will represent the first piece of Mars to be returned to the planet’s surface (Fig. 3a). The intensity target design incorporates an intricate maze (Fig. 3b), and the clear polycarbonate space suit visor material is backed by a distinctive Geocaching trackable marker (Fig. 3c).
