Do dust bunnies form normally in microgravity?
On Earth, dust, hair, and other assorted debris clump up into fuzzy lumps known as dust bunnies, which shelter parasites, set off allergies, clog air filters, and impede electronic-device cooling.
In microgravity, where dust et al. have no tendency to settle towards the ground, do dust bunnies still form as they do in macrogravity? Are there measures taken to mitigate their formation?
