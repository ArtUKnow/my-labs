How is bismuth vapor condensation managed in high-power vacuum arc thrusters (VAT) to prevent short-circuiting magnetic coils?
In a conceptual Nuclear Electric Propulsion (NEP) design utilizing a Bismuth Vacuum Arc Thruster (VAT) with electromagnetic focusing, bismuth is vaporized/ionized and accelerated out of the nozzle.
Because bismuth is solid at room temperature and dense (unlike Xenon), there is a potential issue with bismuth vapor expanding and re-condensing on cold surrounding structures, such as the magnetic focusing coils or spacecraft surfaces.
Power input: ~150 kW to thruster
Propellant: Bismuth (solid rods fed into arc)
