At what distance does radio communication become unusable?
How far away from Earth could we reliably communicate using radio?
I'm thinking that a primary factor would be how much RF power could be kept in a "tight beam" and accurately pointed.
Assumotions:
Users do not care about lag time between sending and receiving.
Using present technology for sending and receiving.
"Reasonable" power is available for use. No harnessed stars or fusion generators.
Large antennas of present construction capability could be placed well away from the noise of Earth.
Do not care about data rate.
