How to compute light time correction for eclipse entry/exit timings?
I used a typical geosynchronous orbital parameters and generated lunar shadow penumbra entry and exit timings using OREKIT and GMAT. GMAT has an option to include light-time correction (also for stellar aberration). The results generated by OREKIT are matching with GMAT when light-time correction is disabled. It is clear that OREKIT computes the timings by considering the geometry only. There is a brief on light time correction in this link https://naif.jpl.nasa.gov/pub/naif/toolkit_docs/C/cspice/spkezr_c.html .
