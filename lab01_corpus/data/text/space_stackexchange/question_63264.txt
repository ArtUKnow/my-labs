These ions exit the nozzle at high speed along the axis of the exhaust plume. If this stream is discharged in a planetary magnetic field, positive and negative ions will be accelerated in opposite directions, at right angles to both the external magnetic field and the plume’s axis.
Acceleration of charged particles will generate an electric field across the plume, and will also generate electromagnetic radiation.
Generation of an electric field by an ion stream passing through a magnetic field is demonstrated by a Magnetohydrodynamic generator
Source:https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetohydrodynamic_generator
Does this process generate a detectable electromagnetic signature which would allow remote sensing to identify the rocket’s design, distance or velocity?
Given Jupiter’s strong magnetic field, would this process have any significance for space exploration using chemical rockets in Jupiter’s vicinity?
