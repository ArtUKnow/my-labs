Why is my apoapsis/periapsis prediction obtained by sgp4 so inaccurate?
I wanted to use sgp4 to predict the apoapsis and periapsis as well as the orbital energy. Since I obtain the velocity vector as well as the position vector of sgp4, I should be able to
get the orbital energy out of the velocity with
$$
\epsilon = \frac{v^2}{2} - \frac{\mu}{r}
$$
where v and r are the parameters I have obtained from sgp4, and mu is mu = 398600.4418 km^3/s^2.
get the semimajor-axis from this
$$
\epsilon = \frac{-\mu}{2a}
$$
and then use the angular momentum $$
L = r \times v
$$
to end with the eccentricity
$$
e = \sqrt{1-\frac{L^2}{\mu \cdot a}}
$$
(since its elliptic)
In python, it looks like this:
mu = 398600.4418
r_mag = np.linalg.norm(r, axis=1)
v_mag = np.linalg.norm(v, axis=1)
energy = v_mag**2 / 2 - mu / r_mag
h = np.cross(r, v)
h_mag = np.linalg.norm(h, axis=1)
a = -mu / (2 * energy)
eccentricity = np.sqrt(1 - (h_mag**2 / (a * mu)))
Finally:
apoapsis = a * (1 + eccentricity) - 6371
periapsis = a * (1 - eccentricity) - 6371
Now, my result is in the right magnitude, but the prediction is off by 10km in the apoapsis alone just for prediction 24 hours later. Where did I go wrong? What am I missing? Thanks in advance.
