Are there any laws in place for a potential lunar territorial dispute?
Considering the limited number of sites of interest to both the Artemis program and the International Lunar Research Station (ILRS), it would seem more than likely that both projects, and by extension the superpowers behind them, would be in a space race to get there before their counterparts. Due to the lack of current jurisdiction, basically whoever gets there first has the right to operate.
But what's stopping another state from deliberately setting up camp right next to yours? In the Artemis Accords, signatories endorsed the idea of "safety zones" to require communication between parties operating in the same area, but the major players in ILRS (China and Russia) aren't likely to be part of the treaty anytime soon.
Additionally, I wonder what are the legal implications for members of both projects (currently only the UAE)?
