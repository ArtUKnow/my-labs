A related question could be, "How much denser could Earth's atmosphere be before rockets wouldn't work?" For instance, if Earth's atmosphere were as dense as Venus, chemical rockets would have a very tough time getting into orbit.
On Venus, rockets get a 10% break on gravity compared with Earth, but Venus's atmospheric density is much higher.
