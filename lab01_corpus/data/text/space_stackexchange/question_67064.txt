Did the first stage of Vulcan-Centaur explode after separation?
Yesterday Vulcan Centaur launched for the second time to get certified for national security missions. The launch had a widely discussed anomaly with one of the solid rocket boosters. However there seems to have possibly been a second anomaly later in the launch during first stage separation.
The SRB anomaly occurred about thirty seconds into launch, and continued through SRB separation at about T+0:02:20. First stage separation occurred at about T+0:05:00. The possible anomaly that I am referring to occurred just after first stage separation, several minutes after the SRB anomaly.
During the first stage separation, ULA's livestream had already swapped to the animation, while the NSF livestream still had its cameras focused on the rocket. In their livestream, something that looked like an explosion was visible. However, I haven’t seen any mention of it, and most commentators were focusing on the earlier solid rocket issue. Was the apparent explosion after first stage separation a nominal event? Did the FTS trigger or maybe the first stage experience a RUD after separation?
