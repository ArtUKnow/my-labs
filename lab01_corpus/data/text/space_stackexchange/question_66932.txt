Microgravity before re-entry on the Polaris Dawn mission
While the astronauts were still in orbit preparing for atmospheric reentry there was already a clear acceleration noticeable.
I'm linking to one of the many YouTube copies of the original X live stream (here the "NASASpaceflight" enthusiasts) because I can have a URL containing a timestamp:
This is the beginning of a sequence in which a tethered pen held by the astronaut on the left floats with clear and significant acceleration "upward" upon release, as if it were a helium balloon on Earth. Other items are also moving "up". This is different from the free fall at the beginning of the mission when the "zero gravity indicator" is released and floats without heading in any particular direction.
Clearly, there is some, as I suppose, deceleration. The X live stream commentary, although not very clear, seems to indicate that the deceleration burn is going on. On the other hand, the live images of the nose cone do not show any thruster action.
Also, would that deceleration be so weak?
