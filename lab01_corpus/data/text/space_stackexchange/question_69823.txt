Flight ready hardware for Mars
SpaceX and its founder, Elon Musk, intend to create manned mission to Mars by the end of the decade using their new Starship spacecraft and launch system. This seems highly implausible, given that SpaceX has only developed early prototype versions of this booster, which, as of this post are far, far away from demonstrating the launch capability that SpaceX has touted for it. Starship is supposed to be a fully reusable spacecraft and launch system capable of quick turnaround somewhere in the order of 24 to 48 hours from recovery to re-launch. The company is a great deal away from being able to demonstrate this claim, if ever. But in the event that they can do so, it seems to me that this represents a mere first step in about a five step process to send humans to Mars, with each step being exponentially more difficult.
The heavy lift vehicle necessary to boost large payloads into orbit of both crew and supplies for a planned trip to Mars.
An orbital staging and refueling area for prepping the mule for a flight to Mars, as well as processing Martian arrivals on return flights.
‘The Mule’ - A dedicated deep space capable interplanetary spacecraft, which would be used to ferry crew and supplies to and from Mars.
An arrival staging area in orbit around Mars, serving as the end point for the mule’s deep space flight and staging for Martian descent and landing on the red planet.
Literally, hundreds, even thousands, of flight ready implements, ranging from reentry and decent vehicles, planetary landers, Martian habitats, vehicles on the surface of Mars, to spacesuits, hydroponics labs - virtually everything needed for a sustained human presence on Mars.
My question is whether anything in the latter categories is past the initial concept work stage i.e. they’re actually making flight ready hardware that will be ready for Starship when it is in an acceptable stage to fly such missions? Would city equipment be ready for a practical test of its capability in the manner that Neil Degrasse Tyson once suggested i.e. setting up a mock Martian base in the dry inland valleys of Antarctica?
