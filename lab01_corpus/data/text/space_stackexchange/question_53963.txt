and references and shows images of ECOSTRESS:
ECOSTRESS (Ecosystem Spaceborne Thermal Radiometer Experiment on Space Station) is an ongoing scientific experiment in which a radiometer mounted on the International Space Station measures the temperature of plants growing in specific locations on Earth over the course of a solar year. These measurements give scientists insight into the effects of events like heat waves and droughts on crops.

Question: How does ECOSTRESS measure both temperature and emissivity simultaneously?
It's a hard problem because a radiated spectrum will be the product of the Planck distribution and the spectral emissivity, and it is looking at the ground through Earth's atmosphere which is both radiating and absorbing thermal infrared light with its own complicated spectrum.
