Why do the New Shepard capsule windows appear to produce so much glare and reflection?
After watching several videos recorded inside Blue Origin's New Shepard capsule, I noticed that the large windows often show significant reflections and glare from the cabin interior.
Given that one of the main attractions of the flight is the view through the windows, I was surprised by the amount of reflected light visible in many recordings.
Are there technical reasons why the windows cannot be optimized further for reflection reduction?
For example:
Do structural or thermal requirements limit the use of anti-reflective coatings?
Are there durability or safety concerns?
Is the effect mainly caused by camera exposure and not as noticeable to passengers?
I am interested in the engineering constraints involved rather than business or marketing considerations.
