Study of selection higher stages for a multistage rocket
Will it be correct to say that the thrust produced by the first stage is the ultimate limiting factor for a rocket's ability? The logic behind this thought is like this: Higher quantity of its propellant to achieve longer firing time & consequently higher altitude, adds to the "payload" (total weight / mass to be lifted by first stage) and limits the altitude to which it can travel. The difference between first stage's lifting capacity, and the actual weight of the total first state (inclusive of propellant tanks etc.) is the "available" margin to add higher stages.
