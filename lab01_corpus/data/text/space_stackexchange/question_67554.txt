Would it be possible to use a Cygnus resupply spacecraft as a temporary space station?
The Cygnus spacecraft used for resupply missions to the ISS is built by Orbital Sciences. The Mission B variant is due to fly next year and has an internal volume of 36 cubic metres, (1,300 cu ft) and a design life of 2 years.
Would it be possible to avoid the development problems of designing a new station and use an off-the-shelf one of these as a stop-gap space station if Axiom et al don't have their space stations available when the ISS is deorbited?
Cygnus has its own power supply but probably not life support, that would have to be provided by visiting craft as is planned for the Haven-1 station.
I notice this idea is not in the CLD proposals.
