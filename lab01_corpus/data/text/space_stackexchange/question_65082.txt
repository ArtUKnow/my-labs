Misting/Fogging Nozzles as Rocket Injectors
Misting and Fogging Nozzles are nozzles often used in irrigation and cooling. They basically use high pressure to break the liquid into tiny droplets achieving really great atomization. I think these kind of nozzles are also used in those hose heads where you change the output. It creates a pretty good example of how the rocket injector plate would work when using misting and fogging nozzles.
Now my question is why people aren’t using it. Why is it a bad idea? The only problem I see is that flow is pretty spread out instead of directional, but that is easy fix and it is designed to have a wide spread to cover a larger area. I think it has better atomization than the usual conical spin injectors. It is also simpler.
