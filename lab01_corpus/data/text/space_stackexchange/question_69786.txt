I found several articles estimating the duration of a low-thrust maneuver. Some assume very little maneuvers (e.g. here), but in my case, the change of true anomaly may be arbitrarily large. Some require to solve differential equations, but I just need a quick estimation rather than a very precise value. This is because I will need to perform this estimation a very large amount of times in a code, so a 5-10% error in the estimation is more suitable than a long computation time.
If possible, I would like to change the radius of the low-thrust satellite at the same time as I correct its phase, and not estimate them separately to add them. How can I estimate the total time for this type of maneuvers?
Also, I should note that I am in a very simple configuration where there are no perturbations applied to the trajectories, I am only assuming a two-body model.
