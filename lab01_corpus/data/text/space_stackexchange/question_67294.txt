To be clear, I am not looking for pictures OF the astronauts in training. I’m looking for the pictures of rocks and soil that they took as practice during training, especially anything from the ALSCC. Maybe some ALSCC stereo pairs from Idaho, or a Hasselblad panorama from Arizona?
