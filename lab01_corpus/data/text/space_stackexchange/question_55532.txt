Solid metal fuel for nano satellite thrusters
A South African company is developing a solid metal fuel thruster that was initially researched by NASA but was not further developed,
an electric reaction could be used to vaporise solid metal fuel, a process which then created a jet of fast-moving plasma that could propel a satellite
They hope to develop a system that can,
move stuff around in space with cheap and abandoned iron ore or other metals.
