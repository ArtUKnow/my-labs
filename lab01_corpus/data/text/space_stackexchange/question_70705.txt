As of August 2020, the ISS has conducted 27 collision avoidance
maneuvers since its initial launch in 1999 and is trending upwards
with time. The class of debris most dangerous to the US Orbital
Segment are those between 1-10 cm.[3] The population of debris in this
size range is significant and difficult to track accurately with
current methods, meriting further research.
