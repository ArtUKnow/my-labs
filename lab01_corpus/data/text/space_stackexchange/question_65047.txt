What happened to Flexcraft?
The DSH Flexcraft seems like a great complement to “traditional” EVAs. Interior environment would be “shirt sleeve”. Astronauts could “scratch where it itches”. It would share a common atmosphere (pressure and composition) with the ISS so no pre-breathing would be required (and, potentially) rescue would be facilitated without equalization or airlock.
Although the design was part of the Deep Space Habitat project. The concept seems to Interface nicely with the Mobile Servicing System (MSS) on the ISS
NASA - http://www.nasa.gov/pdf/464826main_SEV_Concept_FactSheet.pdf
An extension of this design approach is the Multi-MissionScience Exploration Vehicle (MMSEV)
What happened to these concepts? They seem like the way of the future. The way of the future. The way of the future…. (apologies to Aviator)
