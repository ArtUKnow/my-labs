Why are the landing legs on the proposed Starship HLS so small?
A more stable design would be landing legs that extend outwards from close to the top of the cylinder and have a brace bar that locks in place once they are fully extended.It would like kind of like a flying buttress but with a lower support bar. The purpose would be for stability on the Lunar terrain and to prevent the HLS from tipping over which is a risk during a botched landing. If that thing does fall over, there is no way to right it and the astronauts would be stranded.
