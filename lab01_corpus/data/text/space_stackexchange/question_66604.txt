Understanding this LeoLabs plot of (the most recent) Long March upper stage breakup debris field
We’re actively monitoring and analyzing the breakup event in #LEO involving a Chinese rocket body, CZ-6A. Our radar data indicates this event occurred on 6 August at ~20:10 UTC at ~810 km.
It resulted in at least 700 debris fragments and potentially more than 900.
Tweeted August 9, 2024 https://x.com/LeoLabs_Space/status/1821674976245670386
The upper plot shows Doppler radial velocity vs time, and the lower plot shows range vs time. About 1700 seconds (28 minutes) of continuous contacts is shown as tight curved bands of color dots. Also shown are gray and black solid smooth lines presumably representing trajectories of single, known objects.
An idea of the systems likely to have produced this data can be gleaned at https://leolabs.space/radars/
