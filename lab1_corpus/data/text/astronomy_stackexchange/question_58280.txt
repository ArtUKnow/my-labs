How can we hear meteors? How can a material vibrate and produce sound in response to radio waves?
I first heard about this phenomenon in the April 14, 2017
Science.org news item New theory may explain the ‘music of the meteors' Electric fields could launch radio waves that generate sound. Basically it says that meteors produce very low frequency radio waves that some materials can resonate with, producing vibration and consequentially sound waves.
Well, how does that work? How can some material resonate with radio waves and vibrate?
