Why is the absolute magnitude of Venus only slightly dimmer than its peak apparent magnitude?
In the Wikipedia article for Venus, I realized the planet's absolute magnitude is -4.4 which is only slightly dimmer than its peak apparent magnitude of -4.92.
Why? The apparent magnitude denotes the planet's visual brightness as seen from Earth (a distance of about 42,000,000 km here), while the absolute magnitude denotes its brightness if it was in a distance of 10 parsecs (32.67 light-years).
