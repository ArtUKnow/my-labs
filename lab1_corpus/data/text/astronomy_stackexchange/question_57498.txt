What would the Big Crunch theoretically look/feel like?
In the Big Crunch theory, the expansion of the universe reverses, resulting in all matter contracting into a single point. Imagine you are alive at the time of this event. What would the sky look like? Would you feel anything? Basically, what would the general experience of it be like? If you die in the process, also consider how it would be different if you are immortal (ie. just observing, whether you have a physical body or not).
