Why can't lunar regolith support plant growth in the same way as Earth's soil?
Earth's soil can naturally support plant growth because it contains water, organic matter, microorganisms, and nutrients that plants can use. Lunar regolith, however, formed under very different conditions and lacks many of the characteristics commonly found in terrestrial soil.
