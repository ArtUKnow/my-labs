Why did it take until 1992 to discover exoplanets if amateurs have discovered exoplanets?
The first exoplanet system (Lich) was discovered in 1992 using the pulsar timing method. The first exoplanet to be discovered with the transit method was HD 209458 b in 1999. According to https://astrodennis.com/, amateur astronomers have found or verified several transiting exoplanets, though mostly only hot jupiters, using sub-meter telescopes and software to analyze DSLR data. Some amateurs (https://www.spectrashift.com/) have even used radial velocity detection.
Sensitive DSLRs and auto-tracking telescopes were not available to the public until recently, but when many professional astronomical observations involve plotting the brightnesses of stars over time, why were not even hot Jupiters noticed before the 90s?
