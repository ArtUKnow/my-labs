Are these stars in my telescope or dead pixels?
I recently put my DSLR camera into a 20cm reflector telescope to test the remote I got for the camera (since pressing the button physically created a lot of jitter). For context I'm working on building my own astrophotography setup for fun; yes it will not be nearly as high-quality as one I could purchase but I'm doing this out of "I want to be able to say I built the thing" not "I can take really cool pictures" since I already have access to an observatory.
On ISO 1600 and with ~2s shutter time and without a clock drive on the telescope, I captured this image of Mars at around 10:00 PM on 19 April 2024 in an area with some light pollution:
It isn't the best shot of Mars and it hasn't been stacked or anything but the interesting part was the red/blue specks in the background (hard to see on low brightness and are quite small; recommend turning brightness all the way up and zooming in to see). These specks vaguely resemble the stars I saw through the telescope while aligning the camera to Mars, but I couldn't immediately match them up to anything on Stellaris and the variance in color was surprising (some red, some grey/green, some blue).
Would I be correct in calling these stars? Or are they dead pixels in the camera, or are they something else?
