Calculate sun brightness as a function of sun position (zenith angle) throughout the day
I'm doing some ecological modelling using a dataset of observations of wildlife taken at a bunch of different locations and times. I want to assess the extent to which these observations are correlated with how much sunlight there was at the respective time and place. So far I have found that the R suncalc package can calculate the sun position (altitude and azimuth angles) at a given place and time, but this is not entirely what I want because many (negative) altitude angles correspond to the same amount of sunlight (zero).
I would like something that can take the altitude and azimuth angles, and/or the location (latitude+longitude) and exact time (date+time of day), and generate a measure of how much light would be expected to reach the Earth's surface on a clear day. To keep it relatively simple, let's assume that clouds or other atmospheric forces are not relevant. I really only need something that works as a relative measure, and anything that strongly indicates the actual amount of sunlight present in a given place and time will work.
