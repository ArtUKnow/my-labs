Are any Population III stars red dwarfs?
Everything I read about Pop 3 stars says that they were hugely massive stars that died very, very rapidly (relative to stars today), and so it may not be actually possible to find these stars, even with the power of JWST (though there have been reports of possible candidates).
My question is, did the very early universe preclude red dwarfs from forming during this time? If a red dwarf could form early on, they'd still be around today, since their lifetimes are in the trillions of years. So if Population III red dwarfs do exist, we should be able to find them, even nearby, right? Or are only creation of massive stars possible during the dawn of the universe?
