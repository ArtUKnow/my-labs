How quickly would these changes happen if the moon were to suddenly disappear?
There's a lot of articles and videos on the topic of what would happen if the moon were to suddenly disappear. Two consequences that seem to come up often are an increase in variation of Earth's axial tilt and the shortening of days as Earth's rotation speeds up. On what time scale would these changes occur? Would the change in Earth's tilt be noticeable on a yearly basis? By the decade or century? How quickly would the Earths days begin to shorten? Would this be noticeable in a life time or be a long term trend over a greater time scale?
