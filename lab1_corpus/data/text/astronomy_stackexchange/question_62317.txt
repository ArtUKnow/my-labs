What major theories regarding the nature of "little red dots" have been postulated?
The James Webb Space Telescope has brought some answers about the early Universe, but unsurprisingly, also new questions. One of them is: "What are these little red dots, which people keep seeing in the photos?". The objects really need a proper name, but it seems that the issue is going to wait until this question is fully answered.
A number of theories about them have been postulated, some more interesting than others. Could we sum them up? While I think they're likely "just" early AGNs in thick envelopes, alternate theories include population III stars and quasi-stars.
