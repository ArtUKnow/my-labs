What would Uranus look like from light years away?
It has been suggested that at least some of the "super-puff" planets may actually be ordinary planets with rings. Saturn, for instance, may appear as a "super-puff" to an alien astronomer light years away. Uranus also has rings, albeit less extensive than Saturn's.
