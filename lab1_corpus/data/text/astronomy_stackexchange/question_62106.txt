Does the Sun rises exactly from the East and sets exactly in the West at Equator on Equinoxes?
All solar (and shadow) calculators online seem to suggest that the Sun rises perfectly from East (90° clockwise from North) and sets perfectly in the West (270° clockwise from North) on Equinoxes at the Equator. But since the Earth has a tilt of 23.44°, shouldn't the Sun rise somewhere in the North-East (66.56 clockwise from North) and set somewhere in the South-West (246.56° clockwise from North) during the Spring Equinox and then again something similar but opposite during the Autumn Equinox?
The Sun should follow the path of the Ecliptic which is at an angle to the Earth's Equator.
