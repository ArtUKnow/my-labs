Update:
Following a comment I had a closer look on the annotations on the JPL site.
For the inner 8 moons, mean elements are given w.r.t. their local Laplace planes but the columns R.A., DEC. are not the moon RA/DEC it actually gives the direction of the normal of their Laplace-plane w.r.t. ICRF.
For the remaining moons the table lists mean ecliptic orbital elements. Ecliptic is tilted by obliquity = 23.4392811° compared to ICRF.
To transform between Laplace and ICRF I'm following
derive state vectors $\vec{r},\vec{v}$ at time $t=$J2000 from the Laplace orbital elements
rotate both around the x-axis by 90°-DEC
rotate the results around the z-axis by 90°+RA
transform back to orbital elements in ICRF using ref. time $t=$J2000
Ecliptic case is included using RA=270°, DEC=66.560699°.
