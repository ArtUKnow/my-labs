Theory regarding fate of the Universe
I am not from astronomy/astrophysics background but I like to study these subjects whenever it is possible.
The other day I was reading the theory of everything and a thought struck. The idea was that:
After finite time stars which are capable of becoming black holes (Chandrasekhar's limit) will become black holes. These black holes will merge and form something that is way bigger than supermassive black holes.
This thing will collapse under its own gravity and that sounds similar to Big Bang theory.
The idea is that this black hole will contain infinite matter and the Universe will recycle. I am aware of theories such as Big Crunch, Big Chill etc.
Problems with the theory:
Due to expansion of the universe black holes will separate and will not merge but it depends on the rate of expansion and gravitational pull of these objects.
Hawking radiation will evaporate some of these black holes.
