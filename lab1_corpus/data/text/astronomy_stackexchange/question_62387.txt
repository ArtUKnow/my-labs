Measuring the Mars Earth Parallax
My question is about the derivation and the computational process through which Cassini/Richer arrived at the distance between the Earth and Mars. In 1672 Cassini set up an experiment to measure this distance by the parallax method. He in Paris (P) and Richer in Cayenne (C) off the north coast of Guiana in South America made a set of synchronous measurements. Both determined their latitudes, and measured the difference in their longitudes. This enabled them to determine their geographic location from which they computed the arc and chord distances between P and C. Now comes in Mars (M); they synchronously determined its location with respect to the fixed stars.
