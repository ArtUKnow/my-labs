To get the right ascension, I use this algorithm:
Find the UT and Greenwich calendar date corresponding to the local time and date
Find the corresponding Greenwich sidereal time
. . . hence find the local sidereal time
Subtract the decimal hour angle from the LST. If the answer is negative, add 24. This is the right ascension.
To find the Local Sidereal Time I use the user's longitude and GST to get it:
Convert GST to decimal hours.
Convert the geographical longitude in degrees to its equivalent in hours by dividing by 15.
Add this to the GST. Bring the result into the range 0 to 24 by adding or subtracting 24 if necessary.This is the local sidereal time (LST).
