Can elliptical galaxies start forming stars again?
Will elliptical galaxies eventually restart star formation? If they still have any gas, will that gas eventually cool enough to form new stars? If not, will they gain new gas from the intracluster medium?
If so, what would the resulting galaxy look like? Would it be a blue elliptical galaxy? Would it stay in the resulting state long enough to be observed, or would it go through its cold gas and quickly become quiescent?
