Why are my calculations for the Moonrise time always incorrect?
This question is considered a continuation of the my previous question.
I am following the article stjarnhimlen's Computing the Moon's rise/set times tutorial to calculate the moonrise and moonset times.
In the previous question, the issue with my incorrect results was due to not iterating the transit time, and after I iterated it, the results became correct.
Therefore, I moved on to the next step, which is calculating the moonrise and moonset times, and I started by calculating the moonrise with iteration to make the results accurate, just as I did with the transit time.
Steps of the first approximate calculation:
1- Calculating the declination at the transit time (after iteration).
2- Calculating cosine of local hour angle and moonrise hours using the following equations:
$$
\cos(\text{LHA}) = \frac{\sin(h) - \sin(\text{latitude}) \cdot \sin(\text{declination})}{\cos(\text{latitude}) \cdot \cos(\text{declination})}
$$
$$
\text{LHA (in hours)} = \frac{\arccos(\cos(\text{LHA}))}{15}
$$
$$
\text{Moonrise (in hours)} = \text{Transit (in hours)} - \text{LHA (in hours)}
$$
where: h = 0° Because I am using the topocentric declination.
I repeat the previous steps with calculating the declination at the resulting moonrise time, and the iteration continues until the difference between the previous and current moonrise times is less than one minute.
Example 1: At: October 2, 2025
Location: Lat: N 24° 41' 41.89" Long: E 46° 43' 26.86"
My transit time result: 20h06m, Stellarium: 20h06m
My moonrise time result: 14h48m, Stellarium: 14h37m (There is an error of about 11 minutes).
Example 2: At: October 15, 2025.
Location: Lat: N 24° 41' 41.89" Long: E 46° 43' 26.86"
My transit time result: 7h09m, Stellarium: 7h09m
My moonrise time result: 0h29m, Stellarium: 0h12m (This time, here is an error of about 17 minutes).
