Odds of hitting a star with a "laser" shone in a random direction
In this What If, Randall answers what would happen if we shine a laser in a random direction.
When it comes to hitting a star, he says only that it is near impossible to hit one in our galaxy, even if aiming.
I want to know though, if the beam was completely confined (zero divergence) and the photons went on to the visible edge of the universe, what, then, are the odds of it hitting a star?
Same question for other objects. If it's unlikely to hit a star, what about a galaxy or nebula? If likely*, what about a planet?
*(I doubt it is likely)
