Have we missed essential gravitational redshift measurements on S2 approach of Sgr*?
The answer to this previous question revealed that the GRAVITY redshift measurements are, in my opinion, not as good fit for the model as they seem to be.
Background:
S2 orbits the supermassive black hole Sagittarius A* (Sgr A*).
S2 gravitational redshift has been measured and reported in this paper Abuter et al. 2018.
New approach:
It appears that the red line in Fig.3 (see the above paper or the previous question) is "the General Relativity model" (quoting the Answer to previous question) and not a fitting of the measurements (black circles with cyan shading) as I initially assumed.
If this is the case then the highest point (at around 240) is clearly out of the expected range (by ~20%).
What is even more striking is that this highest point corresponds to the last measurement on the way to the pericentre on May 4 (see Fig.2) and no measurements were available until May 24, a few good days after the pericentre. [note (a)]
