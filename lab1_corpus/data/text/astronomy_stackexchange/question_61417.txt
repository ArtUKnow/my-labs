If dark energy increased suddenly, how would that affect gravitationally bound systems?
Dark energy is driving the accelerated expansion of the universe, but it's currently negligible on small scales. If its density were to increase significantly (perhaps on the scale of 10^30 times its current value, over a relatively short cosmological timescale (say, much less than a billion years)) over a short period, could it overcome the gravitational binding of galaxies, star systems, or even planetary orbits? Would galaxies themselves begin to disintegrate, and how would that change the cosmic web? What signs astronomers might observe as a early warning of such an event?
