Why is Haumea a triaxial ellipsoid and not an oblate spheroid?
Haumea, a dwarf planet in the Kuiper belt, is distorted due to its fast spin.
I would expect this spin to result in a uniform distortion into an oblate spheroid, so that Haumea would appear circular if viewed from above/below either pole.
However, all depictions show that it is not uniformly extended along its equator. It is a triaxial ellipsoid.
