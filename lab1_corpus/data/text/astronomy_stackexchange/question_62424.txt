Type Ia supernovae as standard candles at high redshifts - possible influence of progenitor age?
In a recent paper published 11 June Pantheon+ supernovae corrected for progenitor age indicate the universe is decelerating, MNRAS, Volume 549, Issue 3, July 2026.
The authors Animesh Sah , Mohamed Rameez , Subir Sarkar claim using Pantheon+ survey data that the absolute brightness of Type Ia supernovae at high redshifts is different (slightly lower) compared to those nearby and that this systematic error results in an apparent acceleration of the expansion of the universe at high redshifts. The graphs and numbers in the paper look plausible to me but I know very little about the intricacies of Type Ia supernovae and stellar evolution.
