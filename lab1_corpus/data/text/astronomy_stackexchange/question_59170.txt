Vocabulary: name of an angle
We can describe the direction of the axis of the Earth with two angles.
The first is the angle between the axis and the norm of the orbit (ε).
The second is the angle P formed
by the major axis of the orbit and the projection of the axis of the Earth onto the plane of the orbit.
At present, ε measures about 23.45◦, P is about 12.25◦ (P is also
the angle which is covered by the Earth between the beginning of winter (21st December)
and the arrival of the earth at perihelion (2nd January).)
Does the angle P have a specific name in astronomy?
