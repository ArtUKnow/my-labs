For us to see an exoplanet transit, what is the maximum angle between that planet's orbital plane and our line of sight?
We can detect exoplanets by watching their transits dim their star. But this is only possible if the orbits of the planet and the line between us and the star are almost exactly coplanar.
How close is "almost exactly?"
