Why does Mars return to conjunction with fixed stars alternating sequences of regular intervals punctuated by much shorter intervals?
79-Year Repeating Cycle in Mars-Deneb Algedi Conjunctions: An Undocumented Pattern?
Background
I've been analyzing the conjunctions between Mars and the star Deneb Algedi (δ Capricorni, RA = 21h 47m 02s, Dec = -16° 07' 38") over 158 years (1877-2035) using orbital calculations based on Keplerian elements. I discovered a remarkably precise 79-year repeating pattern involving alternating sequences of regular (~707-710 day) intervals punctuated by much shorter (~543-552 day) intervals.

Pattern Analysis
Regular Intervals
The normal intervals are remarkably stable at ~707-710 days, with only minor variations (±3 days).
Short Intervals
Periodically, a much shorter interval of ~543-553 days occurs. These short intervals appear with striking regularity:
Intervals between short conjunctions:
#7 (1941) → #15 (1956): 15 years (7 regular conjunctions between them)
#15 (1956) → #24 (1973): 17 years (8 regular conjunctions between them)
#24 (1973) → #32 (1988): 15 years (7 regular conjunctions between them)
#32 (1988) → #40 (2003): 15 years (7 regular conjunctions between them)
#40 (2003) → #49 (2020): 17 years (8 regular conjunctions between them)
#49 (2020) → #57 (2035): 15 years (7 regular conjunctions projected)
Pattern Summary
With extended data from 1877-2035 (158 years), the sequence of regular conjunctions between short intervals reveals a repeating 5-cycle pattern:
Complete sequence:
1877-1894: 8 regular + 1 short (17 years)
1894-1909: 7 regular + 1 short (15 years)
1909-1924: 7 regular + 1 short (15 years)
1924-1941: 8 regular + 1 short (17 years)
1941-1956: 7 regular + 1 short (15 years)
1956-1973: 8 regular + 1 short (17 years) ← Pattern repeats
1973-1988: 7 regular + 1 short (15 years)
1988-2003: 7 regular + 1 short (15 years)
2003-2020: 8 regular + 1 short (17 years)
2020-2035: 7 regular + 1 short (15 years)
Pattern: 8-7-7-8-7 | 8-7-7-8-7 | ...
The pattern repeats every 5 cycles (approximately 79 years):
17 + 15 + 15 + 17 + 15 = 79 years per super-cycle
This corresponds to ~42 Mars orbits and 79 Earth orbits

I have conducted extensive searches across multiple databases and found no academic sources discussing this specific alternating 15/17-year pattern for Mars-Deneb Algedi conjunctions.
This complete absence of documentation is puzzling given that:
The pattern is highly regular and repeats consistently
Mars conjunctions with bright stars have been studied since antiquity
The alternation between 15 and 17 years is perfectly consistent
Additional Context
The consistency of this pattern over 158 years (1877-2035) with perfect repetition every 79 years strongly indicates a fundamental orbital resonance rather than coincidence.
Key observations:
The 79-year super-cycle corresponds to approximately 42 complete Mars orbits (79 ÷ 1.88 = 42.02)
This is also exactly 79 Earth orbits
The ratio 42:79 may represent a near-commensurability between Mars and Earth orbits
Each 79-year cycle contains: (8+7+7+8+7) = 37 regular conjunctions + 5 short conjunctions = 42 total conjunctions
