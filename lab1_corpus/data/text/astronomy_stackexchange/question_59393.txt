What portion of the neutron star mass is ejected during a kilonova?
A kilonova is when a pair neutron stars collide, such as when they are in a binary system and their orbits decay.
When they crash into each other, the violence of the collision creates the kilonova, where a portion of the material is ejected into space, while the rest remains in the new merged object, likely either a more massive neutron star or a black hole.
