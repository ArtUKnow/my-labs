Why do remote sensing satellites have payloads whose bands' resolutions are more than 1 m?
I was working on satellite data from LISS-III (23.5 m), IV (5.8 m), AWIS (54 m) and Sentinel-2 satellite bands (2, 3, 4 and 8). What caught my eye was the fact that their resolution is more than 1 m (high resolution). My doubt is that say for NDVI, or urbanization, crop-studies, etc, why are bands with more than 1 m resolution needed? The same data that can be obtained using a payload with a resolution of 30 m can also be obtained with a payload of resolution 1m too, right? So why is there a need to operate the bands in medium or coarser levels of resolution?
