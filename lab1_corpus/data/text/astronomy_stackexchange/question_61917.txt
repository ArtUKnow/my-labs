No, the "plane of the sky" is not perpendicular to the galactic plane
when looking towards the galactic center. The plane of the sky is the
imaginary, flat plane that is perpendicular to your line of sight to a
star, while the galactic plane is the disk of our galaxy. Because our
solar system is tilted about (60^{\circ }) relative to the galactic
plane, the plane of the sky when looking towards the galactic center
is also tilted at about (60^{\circ }) relative to the galactic
plane.

[Edit] plane of the sky definition in Wikipedia
An imaginary plane that is perpendicular to the line of sight.
Typically this is used as a reference plane for determining the
inclination of an orbital plane of a distant star system
