DIY Newtonian reflecting scope assembly
Having assembled most of the parts needed to construct a simple DIY 200 mm reflecting telescope with 40 mm eyepiece, I wanted to get an idea of the nature of the images before starting. I placed the collecting mirror facing distant trees, tried to estimate 1600 mm (the focal length) by eye, held the small reflecting mirror with one hand at 45 degrees to the larger mirror, and held the 40 mm eyepiece so it captured the image in the reflecting mirror.
This process was tedious and unsuccessful--everything I saw through the eyepiece was blurry. While this was not entirely unexpected, it seems that I should have gotten a few clear and magnified glimpses. Am I missing some crucial ingredient, or do I just need to do precise measurement when actually assembling? Should it be possible to get an idea of the images in this way? Is it possible, for example, that I would have to look at very distant objects for this to work?
