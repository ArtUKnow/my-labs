Are these terms used to refer to the orbits of Neptune and Pluto valid?
The terms are 'poseidocentric' and 'hadeocentric' for Neptune and Pluto respectively.
I found these words on Wiktionary a few years ago, but their definition pages were deleted, probably due to lack of sources. The terms seem to be consistent with terms used to refer to the orbits of other planets, like 'hermeocentric' for Mercury and 'areocentric' for Mars.
