Will all orbits emitting gravitational waves inevitably coalesce?
All orbits emit gravitational waves since there is an asymmetric distribution which makes the quadrupole momentum not invariant
This means that over long time scales, orbiting bodies would emit gravitational waves and as a result, they would eventually coalesce. However, is this always the case? Or are there some orbital configurations where the members don't necessarily coalesce (without ejecting any member of the system)?
