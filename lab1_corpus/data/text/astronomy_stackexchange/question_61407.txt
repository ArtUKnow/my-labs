Could a Dyson sphere trap enough radiation to shift a star off the main sequence?
A Dyson sphere captures most or all of a star’s emitted energy. But if such a structure also traps and reflects radiation back toward the star, could it alter the energy balance at the star’s surface or core? Would this cause the star to behave as if it were in a higher-pressure environment and potentially shift its stellar classification or lifespan? Could this artificially “age” or destabilize the star?
