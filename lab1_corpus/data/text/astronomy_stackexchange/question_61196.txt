Orbital period anomaly between GJ 1214 b / Enaiposha and 55 Cancri e
I know that Kepler's third law (a³/T²) becomes less accurate for planets very close to their stars, but the anomalous difference in orbital periods for 55 Cancri e versus GJ 1214b seem much larger than that loss of accuracy would seem to explain.
55 Cancir e seems to follow Kepler's quite closely:
Orbital period (T) = 0.7365463 days
Semi-major axis (a) = 0.01544 AU
a³ = 3.68E-06
T² = 4.07E-06
About a 10-11% difference.
GJ 1214 b is vastly different:
Orbital period (T) = 1.580 days
Semi-major axis (a) = 0.01490 AU
a³ = 3.31E-06
T² = 1.87E-05
An order of magnitude difference.
Is there an explanation for why GJ 1214 b seems to orbit so much more slowly than 55 Cancir e despite being slightly closer to its star?
