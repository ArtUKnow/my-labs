I am finding effective temperature using the [Ballesteros equation][1] where I am setting B-V = bp_rp (difference between the blue photometric band and the red photometric band) from Gaia DR3 archive. But as for luminosity I can not find any equations that I can use where the data is available at Gaia DR3 archive. So, how can one find the relative size or luminosity of stars from the archive?
