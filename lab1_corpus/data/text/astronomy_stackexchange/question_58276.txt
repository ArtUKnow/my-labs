Are systems of multiple moons orbiting a planet where they cause tides to each other stable on the long term?
The four big Galilean moons - Io, Europa, Ganymede, and Callisto - they are all tidally locked.
They all wobble a bit (and produce some internal heating) as the moons pass each other in orbit, gravitationally nudging one another.
Is this system stable? Can it be maintained indefinetely? And finally, would there be any way in which a similar system will not collapse or decay due to the emission of gravitational waves over time?
