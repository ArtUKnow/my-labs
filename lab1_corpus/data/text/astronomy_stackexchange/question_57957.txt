Why don't stars 0.5<m<0.8 solar masses undergo a helium flash?
Stars between 0.8 and 2 solar masses undergo a degenerative helium flash, per Wikipedia
and these course notes
The cores of stars <0.5 solar masses never heat up enough for helium fusion. In stars >2 solar masses, the core is already hot enough to fuse helium without becoming degenerate first, so no degenerate helium flash takes place; instead, helium fusion begins more gradually.
