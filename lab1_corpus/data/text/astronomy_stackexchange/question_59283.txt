What happens to the kinetic energy of the fusion products generated in the center of the Sun?
In the center of the Sun, four protons combine to produce a He-4 nucleus. During this process, energy is released in the form of six gamma-ray photons, two neutrinos and kinetic energy of the fusion products. The neutrinos escape almost instantaneously to space. The energy of the photons is slowly transferred to the Sun's surface, via first radiation and then convection, in a process that can last more than a hundred thousand years. But what happens to the kinetic energy of the fusion products? Is it also slowly transferred to the surface of the Sun? How?
