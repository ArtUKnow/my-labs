How to cite Kepler-90
I am writing a paper in which data of orbital periods for the known planets of Kepler-90 are used. I got the data from Wikipedia, which provides a table and cites 4 references. Obviously those 4 are a good place to begin. But research papers in this area often cite a much larger number of works. What is correct practice here? I don't want to make my paper have a long list of refs not really relevant. I just want refs to say how the data was obtained.
I understand there are several stages with space-based instruments: the design and launch, the mission itself, the data gathering, and the subsequent analysis. The latter may itself involve more than one group and more than one paper. Is there a standard practice when citing results from such programmes?
