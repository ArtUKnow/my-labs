Would an observer falling into a black hole ever be observable from the outside due to quantum effects?
Classically, objects falling into black holes appear frozen at the event horizon from an outside perspective. But quantum mechanics — especially Hawking radiation and potential horizon fluctuations — complicates this. Is there any theoretical mechanism by which the infalling object’s information or even appearance might re-emerge or be “echoed” to external observers? What does this mean for the principle of unitarity?
