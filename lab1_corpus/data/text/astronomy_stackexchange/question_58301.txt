Can I determine earthly latitude by position of lunar mare
I am thinking that geographical latitude or at least whether you are in the northern hemisphere or southern hemisphere could be determined by the position of lunar mare. For example, if you see that Mare Fecunditatis (Sea of Fertility) is in the south east (5:00 on a clock position) on the face of the moon, then you are observing from the northern hemisphere. However, if the Sea of Fertility / Fecundity is at the north west (11 o’clock position) on the face of the moon, then you are in the southern hemisphere. Is this true? What about if you are viewing the Moon from the Earth’s equator? Could this approach also be used to make an approximate estimation of latitude whether or not you view the Moon after sunset or even during day time when the Sun is up?
