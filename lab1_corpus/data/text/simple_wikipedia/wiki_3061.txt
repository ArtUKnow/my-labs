Cymru Premier
The Cymru Premier is a football league in Wales. It used to be called the Welsh Premier League; before that, it was called the League of Wales. The league was formed in 1992. Some large Welsh clubs play in England's Football League. Some of these Welsh clubs include Cardiff City F.C. and Swansea City A.F.C., who both play in the EFL Championship.
