Florida Panthers
The Florida Panthers are a professional ice hockey team that plays in the National Hockey League (NHL). They play in Sunrise, Florida at Amerant Bank Arena. They were named for the Florida panther, a type of cougar that is considered an endangered species.
The Panthers have made it to the Stanley Cup Finals three times in 1996, 2023, and 2024. They lost to the Colorado Avalanche in four games (1996) and the Vegas Golden Knights in five games (2023). They would defeat the Edmonton Oilers in seven games (2024).
