Nynorsk
Nynorsk (New Norwegian) is one of the two official written standards of Norwegian; the other is Bokmål. It is based on Norwegian dialects and is especially common around Bergen. Ivar Aasen created it during the 19th century as a Norwegian alternative to Danish, which had been written in Norway since the beginning of the Danish-Norwegian union. It was recognized as an official written language and equal to Bokmål by the norwegian parlement in 1885.
