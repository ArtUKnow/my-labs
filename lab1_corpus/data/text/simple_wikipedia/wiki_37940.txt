Middlesbrough
Middlesbrough is a town in North Yorkshire in northeast England. About 135,000 people live there. It is part of North Yorkshire.
The name is often spelt incorrectly as 'Middlesborough' but 'Middlesbrough' is correct. The town is sometimes called Boro by people who live in the town or near it.
Sports
It has a Championship football club: Middlesbrough F.C..
