Major movie studios
Big Five are the United States's major movie production studios which also distribute their product. All of them have historic flagship production facilities near Hollywood.
All Big Five major studios (Universal Pictures, Skydance Motion Picture Group, Walt Disney Studios, Sony Pictures, and Amazon MGM Studios) are parts of much larger business enterprises, with their corporate headquarters offices far from Hollywood.
The group of major studios long numbered seven, but was reduced to six with the fading from importance of MGM in the 1986.
They became five when, on December 14, 2017, Disney announced its deal to buy Fox for $52.4 billion. And back to six again when, on May 26, 2021, Amazon announced its deal to buy MGM for $8.5 billion, and on April 2, 2025, Amazon MGM Studios announced that it releasing 15 theatrically released movies a year in 2026 and expanded to 16 theatrically released movies a year in 2027. And back to five again when on February 27, 2026, Paramount Skydance announced its deal to buy Warner Bros. Discovery for $110.9 billion that is completed on October 6, 2026 .
