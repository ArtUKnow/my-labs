Oksana Grishuk
Oksana Vladimirovna Grishuk (; born 17 March 1972) is a Russian retired ice dancer. She and her partner, Evgeny Platov, won two Olympic gold medals and four world championships.
Early life
Grishuk began training in skating at the age of four. She moved to Moscow in 1986, and studied at the Sport University from 1988 to 1992.
Career
After they won the gold medal in the 1998 Winter Olympics in Nagano, Japan, Platov decided to skate as a professional with a different partner, Maya Usova, so Grishuk began skating professionally by herself.
Amateur competitions
World Championships: first in 1994, 1995, 1996, and 1997
Olympics: gold medal in 1994 and 1998
European Championships: first place in 1996, 1997, and 1998
