The Empire Strikes Back
The Empire Strikes Back (also known as Star Wars: Episode V – The Empire Strikes Back) is a science fiction movie and the second movie in the Star Wars saga. The movie was released in 1980. It includes action, romance and drama.
In 2010, Star Wars: Episode V – The Empire Strikes Back was selected by the Library of Congress into the United States National Film Registry.
Cast

Anthony Daniels as C-3PO
Peter Mayhew as Chewbacca
Kenny Baker as R2-D2
