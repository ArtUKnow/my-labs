LGBTQ
LGBTQ is an initialism that means lesbian, gay, bisexual, transgender, and queer. It refers to the community of people who are not heterosexual, which means to be attracted to the other gender, or cisgender, which means to identify as the gender you were born as.

Not everyone is in agreement what should or should not be covered in the acronym, or which order the letters should go in. It is an umbrella term because it is about many different kinds of people: A gay man and a trans woman, for example, are both MOGAI.
