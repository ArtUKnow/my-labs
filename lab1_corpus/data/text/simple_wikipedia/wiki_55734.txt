Span is the width of a human hand, from the tip of the thumb to the tip of the pinky finger.
1 span := 9 inches
0.2286 m
In Slavic language, the analogue of span is pyad (peti, пядь). It is the width from the tip of the thumb to the tip of the forefinger.
1 pyad := 4 vershok
7 inches
0.1778 m
