High School Musical
High School Musical is a 2006 Disney television movie, starring Zac Efron, Vanessa Hudgens, Ashley Tisdale, Corbin Bleu, Monique Coleman and Lucas Grabeel. Its sequel is High School Musical 2.
This film is produced by Salty Pictures and First Street Films.
The story is set in New Mexico. It is about two high school students, Troy Bolton, who is captain of his school's basketball team, and Gabriella Montez, a shy new student who is good in mathematics and science. Together, they try out for the lead parts in their high school musical. Despite other students' attempts to foil their dreams, Troy and Gabriella persist and inspire others along the way. They also make new friends.

Production
Filming
in Utah.]]
High School Musical was filmed in the summer of 2005. Photography took place in Utah in East High School. High School Musical was filmed on a budget of US$4.2 million.
Music
The soundtrack for the movie was released on January 10, 2006, it was number 133 on the Billboard 200, It sold 7,469 copies in its first week.
In the album's third week, it went to number ten, and it went to number one on the Billboard 200 twice (on March 1 and 22). It had shipped 3.8 million copies as of December 5, 2006. More than three million copies had been sold by August 2006 and it was certified quadruple platinum by the RIAA. As of January 10, 2023 the soundtrack had been certified 5× platinum by the RIAA.
Expanded franchise
Sequels and spin-off
A sequel called High School Musical 2, was released on August 17, 2007, It was shown on Disney Channel in the U.S., and on Family in Canada.
A third movie called High School Musical 3: Senior Year, was released in the United States on October 24, 2008 . Kenny Ortega returned as director and choreographer, and all six main actors returned.
Sharpay's Fabulous Adventure is a spin-off and direct-to-DVD movie starring Ashley Tisdale. The movie follows Sharpay Evans as she sets out to earn a role in a Broadway show following graduation. The film was released as a Blu-ray and DVD combination pack on April 19, 2011.
In early 2016, Disney announced that a fourth movie was "in the works", It was being referred to as High School Musical 4.
Television adaptation
In 2019, Disney made a new television series called High School Musical: The Musical: The Series. It was shown on Disney+ on November 12, 2019. It was made as a mockumentary, it is about a newly-enrolled set of East High students making a stage production of High School Musical.
Stage adaptations
's School of Dramatic Arts production of Disney's High School Musical]]
On August 1, 2006, Playbill announced that the Stagedoor Manor summer theater camp, would be the first venue to make High School Musical on-stage. The High School Musical: The Concert tour started on November 29, 2006 at San Diego, California. The tour ended on January 28, 2007. It was performed in cities in the United States, Canada, and Latin America. The concert had all of the original cast members except for Zac Efron, who was acting in Hairspray. Efron was replaced by Drew Seeley. The concert had the songs from the film, and it had songs from Vanessa Hudgens, Ashley Tisdale, and Corbin Bleu.
"Feld Entertainment" made a global tour called High School Musical: The Ice Tour. It had its world premiere in New York City on September 29, 2007. The cast included 2004 World Junior Bronze Medalist Jordan Brauninger and 2004–2005 Australian national champion Bradley Santer. The show had clips and songs from both the original film and its sequel, High School Musical 2 and it had a special preview of High School Musical 3: Senior Year.
