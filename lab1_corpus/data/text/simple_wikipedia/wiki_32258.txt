Scratch and sniff
Scratch and sniff refers to paper that produces an odor when it is scratched. It is made so a smell comes out when the surface is scratched. Scratch and sniff is mostly used on stickers, but it is also used in books, greeting cards, and boxes. Scratch and sniff stickers are frequently given to children as a reward for doing well in school. They are also used in games, learning, and to let people sniff a product before they buy it.
Scratch and sniff is created by covering the odor in a covering that breaks easily when it is scratched. The coating has many tiny parts that hold the smell inside until they are broken. When the surface is scratched, the tiny parts break and the smell is let out. Because the odor is covered, it can be kept for a very long time because the smell weakens.
