Humour (Commonwealth English) or humor (American English) () is the way that some experiences can make people laugh or feel happy. Most people are able to be amused (laugh or smile at something funny) and have a sense of humor. People can use puns with words that sound similar but have different meanings, or a word that has two meanings. Other examples of humor are satire, saying yes or no when it is not expected, and using different kinds of logic. People of different ages and cultures can find different things humorous. A humorist shares humor with others. A comedian is a type of humorist who is paid to make people laugh by showing up and making humor.
Humor is describing things in a funny way, having the ability to make the jokes.
