Gibibyte
The gibibyte (symbol: GiB) is a unit of measurement in computers and similar electronic devices. One gibibyte holds 1024 mebibytes (MiB). Some people call this number of bytes the gigabyte. This would be considered the binary equivalent to the base-10 gigabyte, which consists of exactly one billion bytes.
The gibibyte is equivalent to 2 30 bytes (1 073 741 824 bytes) while the gigabyte is 10 9 bytes (1,000,000,000 or a billion bytes).
1024 gibibytes make one tebibyte.
Note on memory measuring
While on storage GB effectively differs from GiB; using base 10 in memory is considered an error prone practice, and it's normally troublesome due to convention taken in the information technology market.
Generally GiB and GB are considered equal when measuring all volatile memory (RAM, Cache, vRAM, etc...)
