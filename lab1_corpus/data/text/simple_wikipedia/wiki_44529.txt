Gerard Way
Gerard Arthur Way is an American musician and comic book writer. He is the lead singer and co-founder of the punk rock band My Chemical Romance. He played for the band from their start in September 2001 until they broke up on March 22, 2013, until the reunion on October 31, 2019, thus rejoining Gerard to the band.
He is known for commonly changing his hair color from black to blonde and even a striking red. He married Lyn Z of Mindless Self Indulgence on September 3, 2007. Gerard and Lyn Z announced that they were having a baby in November 2008. Their daughter, Bandit Lee Way, was born on May 27, 2009. She arrived at 2:57 PM and weighed 6 pounds and 5.6 ounces. Gerard has said that both Bandit and Lyn Z are "happy and healthy". Way is also the writer of the comic book series "The Umbrella Academy".
Before My Chemical Romance
He was born on April 9, 1977 in Summit, New Jersey and he attended the School of Visual Arts in New York City and graduated with a Bachelor of Fine Arts degree in 1999.
