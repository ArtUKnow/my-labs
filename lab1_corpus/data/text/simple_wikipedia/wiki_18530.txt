Hailstone
A hailstone is a round ball of ice which falls out of a storm cloud. About 5000 hailstorms per year in the United States of America make hail 0.75 in or larger. The drop grows by collecting many cloud drops. The little drop is blown by a strong wind inside the cloud to where it meets with some extremely cold water drops. These supercooled drops are still liquid water even though the temperature is below freezing. When the little drop mixes with these extremely cold drops, they join, and the little drop has now become a hailstone.
