Dexter (TV series)
Dexter is an American television series created by the Showtime network. It is based on the book Darkly Dreaming Dexter written by Jeff Lindsay. The main character, Dexter, is played by Micheal C. Hall.
The series is about a serial killer named Dexter Morgan, a blood spatter analyst who lives in Miami. He is known as the Bay Harbor Butcher, who kills other killers.
The series was received to high praise by critics and audiences alike for early seasons, but slowly declined into mixed reactions as the show progressed beyond Season 4. Similar reception was shown for the spinoff series Dexter: New Blood and Dexter: Original Sin, but the 2025 series Dexter: Resurrection brought back the large amounts of success brought on by the early seasons of the original series.
Premise
Dexter Morgan is a blood spatter analyst who works for the Miami Metro Homicide Department in Miami. In secret, he is a serial killer, known to the public as the Bay Harbor Butcher, a murderer who is infamous for killing other murderers.
As the Bay Harbor Butcher, Dexter follows a very strict routine. When he discovers a potential target, he follows them and studies their habits and the places they go to. After that, when nobody is around, he kidnaps the victim by injecting them with a knockout chemical named M-99, and brings them to another location. In this place, he often covers the walls with plastic wrap and puts up pictures of the killer's victims where they can see them. Dexter then wakes up the victim using bath salts, and gets them to confess to what they did. Lastly, Dexter stabs them through the heart to kill them, and divides up the body into several pieces, which he bags up and throws into the ocean from his boat.
When Dexter was a child, his mother was murdered in front of him by members of a drug cartel for being a police informant. This implanted deep trauma into Dexter, later manifesting as an urge to kill.
Dexter was adopted by a police officer named Harry Morgan, with his daughter, Debra Morgan, who became Dexter's younger adopted sister. As Dexter grew up, Harry noticed that he was showing dark, violent urges. Frustrated with how ineffective the justice system was, Harry trained Dexter to focus his urges on to the murderers who escaped justice.
Dexter's first murder was on a nurse named Mary. She had been slowly poisoning Harry with chemical injections while he was admitted to the hospital. After getting his father's permission, Dexter abducted Mary and murdered her.
Dexter later joined the Miami Police Department as a blood spatter analyst, soon becoming one of the best in his field. His sister, Debra, become a police officer. Now as an adult, Dexter balances his "ordinary" life with the police department and his girlfriend Rita, with his secret life as the Bay Harbor Butcher.
Characters
Dexter Morgan, played by Micheal C. Hall. Dexter is a blood spatter analyst for the Miami Metro Homicide Department, but also an infamous serial killer known as the Bay Harbor Butcher. Dexter is convinced he has no emotions and is hardly even human; but goes on to develop emotionally in ways he never knew were possible.
Debra Morgan, played by Jennifer Carpenter. Debra is Dexter's sister, and has a constant habit of swearing. She is fiercly loyal to her brother, protecting him from others when questioned about his introverted nature.
Harry Morgan, played by James Remar. Harry is Dexter's deceased father, a formerly respected police detective. In life, he developed "The Code of Harry", Dexter's guide for carrying out his murders. He now appears to Dexter as a mental hallucination during times of conflict.
Sgt. Angel Batista, played by David Zayas. Angel is a kind-hearted and pure friend to Dexter, as well as a very good detective and later, seargent.
Vince Masuka, played by C.S Lee. Vince is Dexter's fellow blood spatter analyst, and often cracks perverted, sexually-driven jokes, even during seemingly innappropriate times.
Joey Quinn, played by Desmond Harrington. Joey is a detective within the department who later suspects Dexter is a serial killer, but eventually chooses not to pursue it out of self-preservation and his love for Debra.
Sgt. James Doakes, played by Erik King. James is a very tough and clever seargent who suspects that Dexter has a dark secret, and is determined to reveal it.
Cpt. Maria LaGuerta, played by Lauren Velez. Maria is the captain of the department, being highly ambitious and devoted to her work.
Rita Bennett, played by Julie Benz. Rita is Dexter's sweet and kind girlfriend, being devoted to Dexter as he treated her better than her ex-husband.
Astor and Cody Bennett, played by Christina Robinson and Preston Bailey. They are Dexter's adoptive children, their mother being Rita.
Throughout the series, Dexter faces off against a number of antagonists. They are as follows;
Brian Moser, played by Christian Camargo. Brian is Dexter's estranged older brother and a cruel serial killer, known as the Ice-Truck Killer.
Lila West, played by Jaime Murray. When Dexter lies about being a drug addict to cover up his life as a serial killer to Rita, he attends counselling meetings where he becomes sponsored by Lila. Lila eventually becomes obsessed with Dexter after discovering his secret life.
Miguel Prado, played by Jimmy Smits. Miguel is the district attorney, and later teams up with Dexter to hone his buried violent urges. However, Miguel murders a political rival just to get ahead, forcing Dexter to go after him.
Jorge Orozco played by Jesse Borrego. A tree-trimmer by trade, Jorge operated as The Skinner, known for skinning his victims alive. He was later hired by a bitter Miguel to kidnap Dexter for him to personally kill.
Arthur Mitchell, played by John Lithgow. Arthur is a family man and charity worker, secretly operating as the infamous Trinity Killer, killing women in patterns of three over the span of decades. Dexter first believes he can learn from Arthur, but eventually realises how dangerous he is.
Jordan Chase, played by Jonny Lee Miller. Jordan is a motivational speaker with a highly loyal audience. In secret, he works with a secret organisation to kidnap and torture women. These victims would later be discovered in barrels and the group would be dubbed The Barrel Girl Gang by the media.
Travis Marshall, played by Colin Hanks. Travis is a devout Christian and a serial killer known as the Doomsday Killer. He believes that the murders he commits under the mental guise of his dead professor will bring about the appocalypse, as described in the Bible.
Issak Sirko, played by Ray Stevenson. Issak is the leader of the Koshka Brotherhood, a Ukrainian crime organisation. He arrives in Miami for revenge after Dexter murder his lover, Viktor Baskov.
Oliver Saxon, played by Darri Ingolfsson. Oliver is a mentally ill man who operates as the Brain Surgeon, infamous for executing his victims before cutting out the anterior insular cortex of their brain, the part which processes empathy.
In the sequel series Dexter: New Blood, Dexter faces new antagonists. They are:
Matt Caldwell, played by Steve M. Robertson. Matt was a reckless and entitled man who although accidentally murdered several people in a boating accident he caused, got away with it and later murdered a deer that Dexter admired, causing Dexter to target him for murder.
Kurt Caldwell, played by Clancy Brown. Kurt is the father of Matt, and later targets Dexter for the murder of his son. He operates as a serial killer nicknamed the Runaway Killer, luring lone women runaways into his basement before killing them following a highly structured ritual.
In the 2025 sequel series Dexter: Resurrection, more killers are introduced as Dexter adapts to his life as a killer in the modern world.
Leon Prater, played by Peter Dinklage. Leon operates a highly secretive club for serial killers across New York, and invites them to discuss their methods and victims of choice. He also has a vault containing many authentic pieces of serial killer memorabillia, including Dexter's own blood slide box.
Ronald Schmidt, played by Marc Menchaca. Known as The Dark Passenger, he kills rideshare drivers by sawing their heads off with barbed wire. He's later targeted by Dexter, noting his use of the "Dark Passenger" moniker, the name that Dexter gives to his urges. Dexter later uses Ronald's identity to inflitrate Leon Prater's club.
Mia LaPierre, played by Krysten Ritter. Mia is a member of Prater's club known as Lady Vengeance, killing the men she lures into her apartment, having been traumatised by being sexually abused in her youth.
Gareth and his twin brother, both played by David Dastmalchian. Members of Prater's club, the pair are known as one serial killer named the Gemini Killer, known for killing in pairs of two. Dexter discovers the Gemini Killer is a pair of twins after targeting one of them, and later discovering his identical twin.
Lowell Sloane, played by Neil Patrick Harris. Another member of Prater's club, Lowell is a serial killer known as the Tattoo Collector, skinning sheds of tattooed skin from his victims.
Albert Walker, played by Eric Stonestreet. A regular seeming family man, Al has membership of Prater's club due to being a serial killer known simply as Rapunzel. He stalks lone women and strangles them, cutting off their ponytails as trophies. He is the only killer targeted by Dexter to escape, due to unforseen timing.
Sequels & Spin-Offs
The first Dexter spin-off is a web-series named Dexter: Early Cuts, premiering in 2009. Using an animated storyboard format, the series shows many of Dexter's earlier murders as a young adult.
Dexter: New Blood is a sequel series that began airing on 7 November 2021, before concluding in January 2022. Set ten years after the events of the original Dexter series, it follows the new life of Dexter Morgan, under the fake name Jim Lindsay in upstate New York. After his urge to kill resurfaces, Dexter struffles to evade capture by the local police department and take down a violent serial killer operating in his midst.
Dexter: Original Sin premiered on 13 December 2024, and ended on 14 February 2025. Set during a flashback as Dexter is on the brink of death following the events of New Blood, the series follows a 20-year old Dexter and the start of his life as a serial killer.
Dexter: Resurrection premiered on 11 July 2025, and ended on September 5th. After Dexter escapes from a hospital to start a new life in New York City, he infiltrates a secret club of serial killers hosted by billionaire Leon Prater, while being pursued by his old friend Angel Batista, who has discovered he is the Bay Harbor Butcher.
A second season of Resurrection is planned to premiere in October 2026.
