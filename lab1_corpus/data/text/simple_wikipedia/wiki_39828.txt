A pogrom is a form of riot that targets an ethnic or a religious group.
In a pogrom, riot ers attack a group of people, usually Jews, because of that group's ethnicity or religion. In a pogrom, the local authorities such as the police do not arrest the rioters and do not help the victims.
Rioters destroy the homes, businesses, and places of worship of the target group. People of the target group are physically attacked and usually some are murdered. A second pogrom followed in the city in October 1905.
Kristallnacht was a 1938 pogrom that affected tens of thousands of Jews in Nazi Germany. It was a major event leading up to the Holocaust.
