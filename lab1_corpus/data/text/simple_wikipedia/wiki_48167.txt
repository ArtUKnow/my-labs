Lindsey Jacobellis
Lindsey Jacobellis (born August 19, 1985 in Danbury, Connecticut) is an American snowboarder. She competed in the 2006 Winter Olympics and won the silver medal. She also competed in the 2010, 2014, and 2018 Winter Olympics, but did not win any medals. In the 2022 Winter Olympics, Jacobellis won two gold medals in snowboard cross and mixed team snowboard cross with Nick Baumgartner.
