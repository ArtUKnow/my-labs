The Electric Company
The Electric Company is an American educational children's television series produced by the Children's Television Workshop (1971-1977) and Sesame Workshop (2009-2011) for PBS in the United States. PBS broadcasts 832 episodes over nine seasons from 1971 to 1977 and 2009 to 2011. CTW (1971-1977) and Sesame Workshop (2009-2011) produced the show at Reeves Teletape Studios (1971-1977) and Kaufman Astoria Studios (2009-2011) Second Stage in Manhattan.
The Electric Company used sketch comedy and other devices to create an entertaining program to help children of elementary-school age get better at reading skills. It was meant for children who had "graduated" from CTW's main program, Sesame Street.
Season 1 is released on October 25, 1971 to April 21, 1972, season 2 is released on October 23, 1972 to April 20, 1973, season 3 is released on October 22, 1973 to April 19, 1974, season 4 is released on October 21, 1974 to April 18, 1975, season 5 is released on October 20, 1975 to April 16, 1976, season 6 is released on October 18, 1976 to April 15, 1977, season 7 is released on January 19 to October 1, 2009, season 8 is released in January 25 to May 7, 2010, and season 9 is released in February 7 to April 4, 2011.
June Angela as Julie (1971-1977 and 2009) was a long-running cast member of the 1971-1977 rock band named The Short Circus. In 2006, the 1977 departed 1975-1977 cast were or was replaced by the 2009-2011 cast playing the Ruiz family and their friends in their gang.
