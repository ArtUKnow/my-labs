Kyle Busch
Kyle Thomas Busch (May 2, 1985 – May 21, 2026) was a NASCAR race car driver. Busch raced in all of NASCAR's U.S. national touring series—the Cup Series, O'Reilly Auto Parts Series, and Craftsman Truck Series. Kyle's brother, Kurt Busch, used to race in the NASCAR Cup series.
Busch won his 100th NASCAR race on July 17, 2011. He was only the third driver in NASCAR history to do this. The other two drivers are Richard Petty with 200 wins, and David Pearson with 106 wins. With over 50 wins in the Nationwide series, Busch had more wins than any other driver in that series. At the end of the 2015 Sprint Cup Series, Kyle Busch won his first Sprint Cup Series Championship.
On May 21, 2026, it was announced that Busch was hospitalized for a sudden illness shortly before the 2026 Coca-Cola 600 at the Charlotte Motor Speedway in Charlotte, North Carolina. He died later that same day at the age of 41. The cause of death was sepsis caused by pneumonia.
