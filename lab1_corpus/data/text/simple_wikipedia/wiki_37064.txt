Giancarlo Fisichella
Giancarlo Fisichella (born 14 January 1973 in Rome, Italy) is a former Italian F1 driver and drove in Formula One from 1996 to 2009 with teams including Minardi, Jordan, Benetton and Sauber, as well as returning to Jordan after its name change to Force India and to Benetton under the Renault name. He is affectionately nicknamed "Fisi" by the F1 community.
