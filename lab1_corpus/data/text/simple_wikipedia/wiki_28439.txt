Koala
.]]
Koala (Phascolarctos cinereus) is a plant-eating marsupial. They live in the eucalyptus forests of eastern Australia. They are related to the wombats.
Koalas are often called koala bears, because a koala looks like a small bear or teddy bear. However, it is not a bear, it is quite a different type of animal. Koalas sleep 20 hours a day due to their low-energy diet, the toxic eucalyptus leaves.
Appearance
Koalas have brownish-grey or silver-grey fur, and a big pink, dark red or/and black nose. They have sharp claws which help them to climb.
Koalas also have finger prints which look the same as human finger prints. Finger print experts have had difficulty in being able to tell if the prints are from a human or koala. there are over 500 types of gum trees in the world, however, koalas only eat the 4-6 types that they prefer.
Chlamydia
Many koalas are infected with Chlamydia. This is affecting the survival of the species. Koalas on French Island do not have the disease, and so groups of them are often moved to the mainland to repopulate some areas. The disease can cause blindness, pneumonia, urinary tract infections and reproductive tract infections.
Additionally, Scientists have been testing new vaccines that help protect koalas from Chlamydia. These usually show promising results in field trials. The data and monitoring of these koalas have allowed a new find in science that contributes to lowering risk of koalas being infected with such disease.
