Serena Williams
Serena Jameka Williams (born September 26, 1981) is an American professional tennis player. She won 23 Grand Slam singles titles in her career, the most all-time among women in the Open era. She is the younger sister of another former world no. 1 professional female tennis player, Venus Williams.
Personal life
The Williams sisters grew up in Los Angeles. She lives in Palm Beach Gardens, Florida, United States with her husband Alexis Ohanian and daughter. She is a Jehovah's witness.
