Luxembourg franc
The Luxembourg Franc was the type of currency used in Luxembourg from 1848 to 1999.
From 1944 to 2002 it had the same value as the Belgian franc, and people were able to use both currencies in both countries. The symbol for the Luxembourg Franc is fr. or F.
On January 1, 1999, the Luxembourg Franc was replaced by the Euro (€). 1 Euro is equal to about 40 Luxembourg Francs.
Coins
There were different types of Luxembourg Francs, like 25 cent, 1, 20, and 50 Francs.
