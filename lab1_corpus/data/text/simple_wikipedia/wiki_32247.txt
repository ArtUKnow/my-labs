Alarm clock
An alarm clock or alarm is a clock that alert an people or group of people at selected time such as 7:00AM. Most alarm clocks can make the sound to wake people up; some alarm clocks can make light or vibration. They can turn off sound or light just press button or handle. A classic along alarm clock has an extra hand or inset dial that how alarm will ring at selected time.
Most newer televisions, computers, mobile phones and digital watches have alarm features that automatically turn on or sound alerts at a selected time.

Many newer mobile phones have built-in alarm clocks that do not need the phone to be turned on for the alarm to ring off. iPhone 2G became the first iPhone model to have an alarm function. Some of these mobile phones can set the alarm ringtone. Music can be downloaded to the phone and then chosen to play for waking.
