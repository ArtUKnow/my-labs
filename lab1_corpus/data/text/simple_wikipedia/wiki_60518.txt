Eddie Izzard
Edward Jonathan "Eddie" Izzard (born 7 February 1962) is an English actor and stand-up comedian. She has acted in movies and television. She is known for Her strange comedy and for being a transvestite. She has also been a voice actor for movies such as Rock Dog, The Lego Batman Movie, and The Wild.
Personal life
Izzard has described herself as "a lesbian trapped in a man's body".
