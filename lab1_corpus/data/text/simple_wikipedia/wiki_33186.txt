Siberia

Siberia is the part of Russia that is in Asia. It covers wide landscapes, and stretches from the Ural Mountains in the east to the Pacific in the west. It also stretches from the Arctic Ocean in the north to the border with China, Kazakhstan, and Mongolia in the south.
The Yenisei river is sometimes called the border between east Siberia and west Siberia. Siberia covers about 70% of all Russian territory, but only 26% of Russians live there. It is one of the coldest places on Earth where people live. 70% of people in Siberia live in cities.
Novosibirsk is the largest city in Siberia, with a population of more than .
Omsk and Krasnoyarsk also have populations of over a million people, while Tomsk and Kemerovo have populations of over 500 thousand.
Tomsk is one of the most beautiful places in Siberia. It is said to have the best education in Siberia.
Omsk has very polluted air. This is quite common in Russia, where civilian protests are less effective than in Western countries.
Siberian people
Many different ethnic groups live in Siberia. They are sometimes called Sibiryak ( Russian: сибиряк) or Sybirak ( Russian: сыбирак, from Polish) people. Most people in Siberia who are not Slavic are Turkic peoples. The first Slavic settlers, from European Russia, arrived in the 17th century.
