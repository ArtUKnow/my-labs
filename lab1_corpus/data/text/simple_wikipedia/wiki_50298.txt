Phosphate
A phosphate is a salt of phosphoric acid. Phosphates are important in biochemistry.
Phosphates have the formula PO 4 3- and a molar mass of 94.973 g/mol. An example of a phosphate is sodium phosphate. Three different types of phosphates are known. They are orthophosphate, PO 4 3- ; metaphosphate, PO 3 2- ; and pyrophosphate, P 2 O 7 3- . They have a combining power of 3.
Structure
Phosphates are made of one phosphorus atom surrounded by four oxygen atoms.
Many phosphates do not dissolve in water.
