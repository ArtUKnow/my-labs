Ojamajo Doremi
, also known as Magical DoReMi is an anime that follows the adventures of three elementary school girls as they help Majo Rika turn back into a human being. The anime was created for girls aged 7-14.
The series aired in Japan on TV Asahi between February 1999 and January 2003, and consisted of four seasons, with a total of 201 episodes. There were also two spin-off seasons and three movies (the newest being Looking for Magical Doremi).
An English dub of the first season was produced by 4Kids Entertainment; it began airing in North America in 2005, retitled Magical DoReMi.
Characters
Doremi Harukaze - A kind-hearted, clumsy girl with red hair. Her sister Pop often annoys her. Her color is pink. Her fairy is named Dodo. Her name in the English dub is Dorie Goodwyn.
Pop Harukaze - Doremi's little sister with pink hair and the fourth witch. She has good behaviour, but she annoys Doremi. Her color is red. Her fairy is named Fafa. Her name in the English dub is Caitlyn Goodwyn.
Hazuki Fujiwara - a friend of Doremi, she is a smart, polite and shy girl, with brown hair. Her color is orange. Her fairy is named Rere. Her name in the English dub is Reanne Griffith.
Aiko Senoo - another friend of Doremi, she is a sporty country girl with blue hair. Her color is light blue. Her fairy is named Mimi. Her name in the English dub is Mirabelle Haywood.
Majo Rika - a witch accidentally turned into a Witch Frog (a green blob) by Doremi. Her name in the English dub is Patina.
Lala - Majo Rika's fairy with blonde hair, who is warm hearted to the Witch Apprentices. Her name in the English dub is Laralie.
Onpu Segawa - The fourth witch. She has violet-colored hair. Her theme color is purple. Her fairy is named Roro. Her name in the English dub is Ellie Craft.
Momoko Asuka - The fifth witch. She has fluorescent yellow hair. Her color is yellow. Her fairy is named Nini.
Hana Makihatayama - The sixth witch. She has lemon yellow colored hair. Her colors are white and lemon yellow. Her fairy is named Toto.
Story
Ojamajo Doremi
The story begins in a town named Misora.
Doremi Harukaze is a third-grader who is interested in magic. After a bad day at school, Doremi wanders into a part of town that is unfamiliar. She then sees an old magic shop called the , and decides to look inside. There she finds Majo Rika. As Doremi looks around, she finds out that it is a real magic shop and that Majo Rika is a witch. When Doremi says this to Majo Rika, Majo Rika turns into a Witch Frog. Majo Rika offers to let Doremi become her new witch apprentice. Doremi, fascinated with magic, accepts.
Doremi becomes a witch apprentice, and practices using magic. She learns what it is like to be a witch from her experiences.
After something very strange happens, Doremi's friends begin to ask questions. Doremi decides to take Hazuki and Aiko to the Maho-dou. At the Maho-dou, they learn what happened with Doremi and also become witch apprentices; they keep it a secret. They then redecorate the Maho-dou with Lala's approval, even though Majo Rika doesn't like it.
The series follows the lives of Doremi, Hazuki, and Aiko as they learn, train, and pass tests to become witches.
Later on Pop also finds out about their secret and becomes an apprentice too.
Ojamajo Doremi
They meet a new witch baby born from a blue flower. Following this they were tasked by the Witch Queen to become the baby's mother. Doremi named her "Hana."
Mōtto! Ojamajo Doremi
The witches meet a new witch named Momoko Asuka, who has returned from the United States of America.
Ojamajo Doremi Dokkān!
Hana wants to be a human alongside Doremi and her friends, so she uses all her magic to grow into a sixth-grader. This breaks her crystals and transforms the Maho-dou magic shop into a crafts shop.
