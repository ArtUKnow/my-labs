Volta (album)
Volta is the sixth album by Icelandic rock singer Björk. The album was released on 1 May 2007. She has said that she wanted to "have fun" while making the music for the album. The style of the album is electronic pop. Five singles were released from it. The first single "Earth Intruders" was released April 2007. Timbaland produced this single.
