Amami Islands
The is a group of islands in Kagoshima prefecture in the Kyūshū region of Japan. It is also known as the .
History
The name of the islands comes from the mythological figure of Amamiko.
It was attacked by the Ryūkyū Kingdom in 1570, but was invaded by Satsuma Domain in 1609.
Amami was occupied by the United States from 1945 to 1953. Other islands include Kikai Island, Tokunoshima, Okinoerabu Island, Yorontou and some small islands make up the Amami Islands.
