Work and money
As early as January 2016, observers point out that many buildings in Chinese cities are empty, and nobody really lives there. Despite the low demand, developers still make apartment and other types of new buildings.
The Chinese government states that the Chinese population is over 1,400,000,000, but Western experts and analysts are skeptical about that. A low estimate, of the number of people in China, is about 350 million.
Since May 2025, a scandal involving well-sold Chinese tampons got people's attention. It contained too much thiourea (致癌物超標). Around 30 women have thyroid cancer.
