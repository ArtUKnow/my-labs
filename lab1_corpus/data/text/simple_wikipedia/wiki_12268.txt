Homestar Runner
Homestar Runner, also called H*R, is an Internet surreal comedy cartoon series that was made using Adobe Flash. It usually gets updated every Monday (but, except for an update on April Fools' Day of 2014 and a few cartoons since, it has not been actively updated since December 2010) with a cartoon, a game, or a Strong Bad Email. The people that made the website are Mike Chapman and Matt Chapman. They are brothers and are called The Brothers Chaps. The cartoons on the website normally show Homestar Runner or Strong Bad, who are the main characters on the cartoon.
Characters
Homestar Runner - The main character of the show. He has no arms and speaks with a speech impediment, making the "W" sound where "R" sounds should be. He is very athletic, but not very smart.
Pom Pom: Homestar's best friend, who only speaks by making bubble sounds. He's a large round orange ball who bounces around to move.
Strong Bad: The bad guy of the show. He is a main character and has his own videos called Strong Bad Emails or "sbemails". His face looks like a wrestling mask and he has boxing gloves for hands.
Strong Mad: Strong Bad's big brother, who is not very smart, is strong and is almost always angry. He has a big square body and no neck.
Strong Sad: Strong Bad's little brother, who is usually sad. He is large, and is white and grey with elephant feet.
Bubs: This character runs a store in the show. He has a blue head and a large mouth, which always has a large smile.
Coach Z: Homestar's coach, who cannot say words the right way. He loves hip-hop music and tries to be a rapper, but isn't very good at it.
The Cheat: Strong Bad's pet. He is small and yellow. He helps Strong Bad cheat at things and makes Flash animations.
Marzipan: Homestar Runner's girlfriend and the only female character in the show. She looks like a hippie broomstick with a ponytail. She plays an acoustic guitar named Carol. She often breaks up with Homestar, but gets back together with him quickly.
The King of Town: The King lives in a big castle and eats a lot.
The Poopsmith: The King's helper. He rarely speaks, using a sign instead, and is usually shoveling poop.
Homsar: A character made after someone misspelled "Homestar" in an email to Strong Bad. He has a weird appearance and says strange things.
Video games
Telltale Games, the makers of video games based on the Sam & Max comic book, made a game called Strong Bad's Cool Game for Attractive People for Windows and the Wii's WiiWare. It's an episodic game with five episodes, and it is made with 3 dimensions instead of 2 dimensions like Homestar Runner is. The first episode was called Homestar Ruiner, and ended with 8-Bit is Enough.
