Ralph Fiennes
Ralph Fiennes (born 22 December 1962) is an English actor. He has won several awards including the Tony Award and was nominated for the Academy Award for his work in Steven Spielberg's movie Schindler's List, and the Genie Award. He was nominated for another Academy Award for his role in The English Patient.
Fiennes is also well known for portraying Lord Voldemort in four of the eight Harry Potter movies. He also played Gareth Mallory / M in the James Bond movies Skyfall (2012), Spectre (2015), and No Time to Die (2021).
In 2024, Fiennes played Cardinal Thomas Lawrence in the mystery thriller movie Conclave.
Family
His parents were Jennifer Lash and Mark Fiennes. His brothers and sisters are Joseph Fiennes, Sophie Fiennes, Martha Fiennes, Magnus Fiennes and Jacob Fiennes. He was born in Ipswich, Suffolk.
