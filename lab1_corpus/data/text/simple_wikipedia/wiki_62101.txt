Kurdish language
The Kurdish language (Kurdish: Kurdî) is a Northwestern Iranic language spoken by the Kurds in an area called Kurdistan, which spans today's southeastern Turkey, northwestern Iran, northern Iraq and northeastern Syria. Kurdish has two main dialects and many subs dialects. The two main ones are Kurmanji and Sorani. Kurdish belongs to the same language group as the Iranic languages. Another well-known Iranic language is Persian. They are considered Indo-European languages .
