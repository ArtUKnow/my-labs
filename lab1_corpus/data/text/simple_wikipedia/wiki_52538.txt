La Liga
The Campeonato Nacional de Liga de Primera División, commonly known as Primera División, or LaLiga, and officially known as LALIGA EA SPORTS (stylized as all caps) for sponsorship reasons, is the men's top professional football league of the Spanish football league system.
With twenty teams every year, Barcelona, ​​Real Madrid and Athletic Bilbao are the teams that have never been relegated.
