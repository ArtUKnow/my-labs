Ennio Morricone
Ennio Morricone (November 10, 1928 – July 6, 2020) was an Italian classical composer famous for making movie scores. He composed the scores for more than 500 movies and television programs. He is well known for composing the scores to Western movies, particularly Spaghetti Westerns.
Morricone received an Academy Honorary Award at the 2007 ceremony for his contribution to movie music. In 2016, he won the Academy Award for Best Original Score for The Hateful Eight. In his career, the composer sold over 50 million records worldwide.
Morricone was born in Rome. He died at a hospital in Rome on July 6, 2020 from problems caused by a fall, aged 91.
