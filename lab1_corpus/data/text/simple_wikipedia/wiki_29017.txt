Canadian football
Canadian football, also referred to as football in Canada, is a team sport. The sport is played by two teams with 12 players on each side during play. The field is 110 by 65 yards (101 by 59 m) and the goalposts are in front of the end zones. The field is usually made with grass or artificial turf. Canadian football is played with three downs and more than one player is able to be in motion before the football is snapped. The other players along the line of scrimmage are not allowed to receive forward passes and cannot move until the play begins once they are in position. Points can be scored by bringing the ball across the opponent's end zone for a touchdown, converting an extra one or two points, completing a field goal kick through the goal posts, or a safety occurs in a team's own end zone, a single when the ball is kicked into the end zone and not returned. Each game is split into two halves of 30 minutes and four 15-minute quarters. If the game is tied after four quarters, the game will go into overtime. A game is won in overtime after a team scores more points in a equal amount of offensive possessions from the opponent's 35-yard line.
The main leagues that play Canadian football are the professional Canadian Football League (CFL), and U Sports, which plays college football. The Canadian Football Hall of Fame is located in Hamilton, Ontario.
