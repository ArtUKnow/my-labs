Nahuatl
Nahuatl is a language spoken by 1.5 million people, mostly in Mexico.
History
Nahuatl has existed since the 7th century CE.
The first people to speak Nahuatl were indigenous peoples in central Mexico. However, most people who speak it now also speak Spanish.
