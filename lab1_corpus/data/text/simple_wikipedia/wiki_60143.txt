Jordin Sparks
Jordin Brianna Sparks (born December 22, 1989 in Phoenix, Arizona) is an American musician, record producer, dancer, model, singer and actress. On May 23, 2007, she won the 6th season of the reality television show American Idol. She was the youngest winner of the talent show. She has released three studio albums Jordin Sparks, Battlefield and the hit album "No Restrictions". She has a vocal range of a mezzo-soprano.
