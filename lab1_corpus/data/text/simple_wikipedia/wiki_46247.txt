Edmund I of England
Edmund I (921 26 May 946), the Elder, the Deed-Doer or the Magnificent, was King of England from 939 until his death. He was a son of Edward the Elder and a half-brother of Athelstan. Edmund was the first king to start his rule with a united England. He was succeeded by his brother Edred. Edmund is buried at Glastonbury Abbey.
