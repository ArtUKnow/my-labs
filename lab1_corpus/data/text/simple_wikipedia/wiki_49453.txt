Tenacious D
Tenacious D is an American rock band formed in Los Angeles, California in 1994. Jack Black is the lead singer, and Kyle Gass is the lead guitarist. Their songs have lots of profanity, and their most popular songs include "Tribute" and "Kickapoo".
