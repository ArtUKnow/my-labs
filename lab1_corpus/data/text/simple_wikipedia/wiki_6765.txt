The Spiders from Mars
The Spiders from Mars were a glam rock band from 1970 to 1973. It was a project created by David Bowie. Bowie himself was the lead singer, playing his alter ego character Ziggy Stardust. Ziggy's 'life' came to an end in a live concert at the Hammersmith Odeon on July 3, 1973. This show was released as a movie in 1983, called Ziggy Stardust - The Motion Picture. A 30th anniversary edition of this movie was released in 2003.
Ziggy Stardust and The Spiders from Mars recorded two albums:
The Rise And Fall Of Ziggy Stardust And The Spiders From Mars (1972)
Aladdin Sane (1973)
Band members
Trevor Bolder - Bass guitar
David Bowie - Guitar, Keyboards, Saxophone, Vocals
Mick Ronson - Guitar, Piano, Vocals
Mick "Woody" Woodmansey - Drums
