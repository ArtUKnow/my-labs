Chad Kroeger
Chad Robert Turton (better known as Chad Kroeger) is a Canadian singer and guitarist for the rock band, Nickelback. He was born on November 15, 1974, in Hanna, Alberta, Canada.
Kroeger is of English, German, Irish, Norwegian, Scottish, Swiss-German, and Welsh descent. Kroeger became engaged to Canadian singer Avril Lavigne in 2012. The couple married in 2013 and separated in 2015.
Nickelback consists of four band members: Chad Kroeger (vocals, lead guitar), Ryan Peake (guitar, backing vocals, piano), Mike Kroeger (bass guitar) and Daniel Adair (drums, backing vocals).
