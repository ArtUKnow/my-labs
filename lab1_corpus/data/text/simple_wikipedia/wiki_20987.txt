Georgia O'Keeffe
Georgia O’Keeffe (November 15 1887 – March 6 1986) was an American artist. She is known for her colorful close-up painting of flowers.
She was born on November 15, 1887 on a farm in Sun Prairie, Wisconsin. O'Keeffe studied art at the Art Institute of Chicago and the Art Students League of New York.
Georgia O'Keeffe married the photographer and art dealer Alfred Stieglitz in 1924. Stieglitz had shown her work since 1916 and helped introduce it to a wider audience.
In 1929 O'Keeffe traveled to New Mexico. She was inspired by the desert and light. She moved there in 1949.
O’Keeffe died on March 6, 1986 in Santa Fe at the age of 98.
Auction record
In 2014, O'Keeffe's painting Jimson Weed/White Flower No. 1 sold for US$44.4 million. At that time, it was the highest auction price for a work by a woman artist. The record was broken in 2025, when Frida Kahlo's painting The Dream (The Bed) sold for US$54.7 million.
