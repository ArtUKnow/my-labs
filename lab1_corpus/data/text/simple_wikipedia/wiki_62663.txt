A puzzle is a problem to make the user think. They are often used for entertainment and can help with logic and problem-solving skills. The user may need to recognize patterns, for example, in colors, shapes, numbers, physics, or symbols, to solve it.
Puzzles, such as wordsearches, crosswords and Sudoku grids are printed in newspapers and magazines, but puzzles can be with objects or even on computers..
