Scarlet
Scarlet is a slight brilliant red color. It is a color of the rainbow. It is redder than vermilion. The color's name comes from the Persian saqirlat.
An early recorded use of scarlet as a color name in the English language dates to 1250.
Meaning of Scarlet
Traditionally, scarlet is the color of flame. People also call the color of the blood of a living person scarlet, even though the actual color of blood is crimson.
Scarlet may represent sin.
Scarlet represents sensual pleasure and fun. Scarlet represents partying.
