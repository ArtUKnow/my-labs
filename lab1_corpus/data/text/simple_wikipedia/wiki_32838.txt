Remscheid
Remscheid is a city in the German state of North Rhine-Westphalia.
It has about 115,000 inhabitants.
Twin towns – sister cities
Remscheid is twinned with:
Ashington, England
Granada, Spain
Kırşehir, Turkey
Mrągowo, Poland
Newbiggin by the Sea, England
Pirna, Germany
Prešov, Slovakia
Quimper, France
Famous people who were born in Remscheid
Wilhelm Conrad Röntgen (1845–1923), physicist who discovered X-rays, the first person to win the Nobel Prize in Physics
Peter Brötzmann (1941–2023), jazz musician
