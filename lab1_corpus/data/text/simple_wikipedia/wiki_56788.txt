Buffy the Vampire Slayer (movie)
Buffy the Vampire Slayer is a 1992 American action horror comedy fantasy movie. It was written by Joss Whedon, and directed by Fran Rubel Kuzui. Buffy the Vampire Slayer starred Kristy Swanson as Buffy, with Donald Sutherland, Paul Reubens, and Luke Perry also playing major roles.
The film is about a high school cheerleader named Buffy, who was chosen by fate to be a Slayer; this mostly entailed fighting and killing vampires.
The movie has elements of horror parody, and goes against the clichés of horror movies.
When the Buffy the Vampire Slayer film was first released, it was moderately successful and received mixed reviews from critics.
The mostly lighthearted-seeming movie would later be remade into a darker, much more popular TV series, Buffy the Vampire Slayer, with mostly different characters and a different tone, dropping almost all of the movie's humorous elements, and with a much more serious-seeming, even dour, Buffy (played by Sarah Michelle Gellar), as opposed to the funner Buffy (as portayed by Swanson).
Joss Whedon, who had created both the movie and the television versions of Buffy the Vampire Slayer, has often said that the television series was more like his original vision of the Buffy universe than the movie was.
