John Denver
Henry John Deutschendorf Jr. (December 31, 1943 – October 12, 1997) was an American musician, known for his singing and guitar playing, as well as a songwriter. He was very popular in the 1970s. He died when the plane he was flying crashed in California.
John Denver began his music career with folk music groups during the late 1960s. By 1974, he was one of America's best-selling performers. AllMusic described John Denver as "among the most beloved entertainers of his era"
On 12 October 1997, John Denver died in a plane crash. He was 53 years old.

Death
On October 12th, 1997, John Denver was piloting his own "experimental ultralight plane" off of Monterey Bay, California. He made an error in calculating his remaining fuel levels and fatally crashed directly into the ocean near Lover's Point, Pacific Grove, after the plane ran completely out of gas. After a thorough investigation completed by the National Aviation Services and supporting law enforcement, it was deemed that he was too confined within the limited space of his plane to successfully engage his reserve tank.
His body was cremated, and his ashes scattered.
