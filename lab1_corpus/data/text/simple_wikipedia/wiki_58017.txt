Bratislava Region
The Bratislava Region () is one of the eight regions of Slovakia. The capital is Bratislava, which is also the nation's capital.
The Bratislava Region has 8 districts: Malacky, Pezinok, Senec and 5 districts of Bratislava (Bratislava I – Bratislava V, which create the city of Bratislava). There are 73 municipalities in the region.
The main rivers in the region are the Danube and Morava. It borders with Austria and Hungary.
