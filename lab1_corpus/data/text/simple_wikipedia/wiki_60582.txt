Conrad Lant
Conrad "Cronos" Lant (15 January 1963 – 25 September 2026 ) was the vocalist and bass player in black metal band Venom. He was born in 1963 in London, England.
Career
Cronos started to sing in 1978 in the band Guillotine. Then he knew Jeffrey "Mantas" Dunn and they founded Venom.
In 1989 Cronos left the band to start a solo work. Venom could not go on without him and they stopped singing in 1993. Then Cronos returned in 1995 and rejoined the band.
Death
On 1 October 2026, Cronos' bandmates Rage and Danté and his son Nathan announced on social media that Cronos had died at the age of 63. No cause of death has been given.
