Donkey Kong
Donkey Kong is a video game character created by Nintendo. He was first seen in Donkey Kong. He is a large gorilla who lives deep in the jungles of his very own island. Since 1994, he has worn a red necktie with his initials "DK" in yellow, his only piece of clothing. He appears as both the hero and villain of the Donkey Kong games.
