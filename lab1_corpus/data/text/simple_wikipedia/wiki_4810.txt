Transcontinental countries are listed in the continent the United Nations has grouped them into. In some cases, it is not obvious which continent a country should be listed in.
Africa
Africa is the world's second-largest and second-most-populous continent. At about 30.3 million km 2 (11.7 million square miles) including adjacent islands. It covers 6% of Earth's total surface area and 20% of its land area. With 1.3 billion people as of 2018, it accounts for about 16% of the world's human population.
