Fuck
Fuck is a common English language swear word. It can be used either as an expression or to mean the act of sex. However, the word fucking is used as an intensifier before a word (e.g. "I fucking hate this game").
It is commonly used to express anger and frustration (e.g. when something is not going in favor of someone). However, the word is offensive to most people.
