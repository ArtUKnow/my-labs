Davina McCall
Davina Lucy Pascale McCall (born 16 October 1967) is an English-French television presenter. She is best known for having presented the early seasons of Big Brother UK through its time on Channel 4.
She was born in London to a French mother and British father. She is the presenter of quiz show The Million Pound Drop Live. She is also currently a judge on the UK Masked Singer.
