TV Tokyo is a Japanese television station in Tokyo, Japan. It is also known as "Teleto," a portmanteau of "terebi," the Japanese word for "television," and "Tokyo." Its main owner is Nihon Keizai Shimbun. It was founded in 1951. TV Tokyo is the smallest of the major Tokyo television networks.
