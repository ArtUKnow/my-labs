James Earl Jones
James Earl Jones (January 17, 1931 – September 9, 2024) was an American actor. He was famous for doing the voice of Darth Vader in the Star Wars movies, for doing the voice of Mufasa in The Lion King and in the 2019 remake,
He was a son of actor Robert Earl Jones. He had diabetes at the time of his death.
