Vaccinium vitis-idaea
Vaccinium vitis-idaea (also called a lingonberry, partridgeberry, mountain cranberry, or cowberry shrub) is a small evergreen shrub from the heather family that makes edible fruits. Lingonberries are a food crop in Scandanavia, and are an ingredient in many traditional Scandanavian meals. They are the main ingredient in lingonberry jam.
