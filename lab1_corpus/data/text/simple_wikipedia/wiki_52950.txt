David Alfaro Siqueiros
David Alfaro Siqueiros (December 29, 1896 in Camargo, Chihuahua, Mexico - January 6, 1974 in Cuernavaca, Morelos, Mexico) was a famous painter and muralist. He was a committed Marxist and his work had a strong political and social intention. He was famous for large murals using the latest in equipment, materials and technique that now can be seen in famous public spaces in Mexico.
