Jupiter (mythology)
Jupiter (Latin: Iuppiter) is the king of the gods in Roman mythology. This was because he had been warned that one of his children would overthrow him.
Battle of the Titans
Then, with the help of the Cyclopes and the Hundred-handed Giants, they declared war on Saturn and the other Titans. Jupiter finally defeated the Titans and they were imprisoned in Tartarus.
Dividing the universe
Jupiter and his brothers divided the universe into three parts, Jupiter obtaining the heavens, Neptune the sea and Pluto the underworld. This is how Jupiter became the king of the gods.
