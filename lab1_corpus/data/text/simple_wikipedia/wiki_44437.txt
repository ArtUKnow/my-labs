Franz Ferdinand (band)
Franz Ferdinand is a Scottish indie rock band from Glasgow, Scotland. The group is named after Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria-Hungary. The band has released six albums, Franz Ferdinand, You Could Have It So Much Better, Tonight: Franz Ferdinand, Right Thoughts, Right Words, Right Action, Always Ascending and their most recent release, The Human Fear.
The band members are Alex Kapranos, Robert Hardy, Julian Corrie, Dino Bardot and Audrey Tait, past members are Paul Thompson and Nick McCarthy.
