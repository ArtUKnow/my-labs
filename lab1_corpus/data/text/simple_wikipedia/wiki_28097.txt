Slashdot
Slashdot (sometimes abbreviated as /.) is a website where people can read news. Many of the things on Slashdot are about computers, the Internet, video games, and politics. Most people who go to Slashdot like computers.
On the Slashdot website, people can comment on the news. Often this leads to interesting discussions with many points of view.
Slashdot does not cost money to read.
