Eintracht Frankfurt
Eintracht Frankfurt is a German sports club from Frankfurt am Main, best known for the football section.
The club plays in the Bundesliga. They have won the German Championship once and German Cup (DFB-Pokal) 5 times (last time in 2018) and the UEFA Europa League twice.
They were founded in 1899. The team colours are red, black and white. They play at Deutsche Bank Park (former Waldstadion). The current captain of the side is goalkeeper Robin Koch from Germany.
The coach of Eintracht Frankfurt is Adi Hütter.
Eintracht has also 18 other sections such as athletics, basketball and ice hockey.
