Joachim Löw
Joachim Löw (born 3 February 1960) is a German former professional football player who formerly managed the Germany national football team after being eliminated by England in the UEFA Euro 2020 Round of 16. He became a World Cup winning manager when his team won the 2014 FIFA World Cup.
