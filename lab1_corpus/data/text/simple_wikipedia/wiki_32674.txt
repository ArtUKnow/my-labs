Wuppertal
Wuppertal is a city in the west of Germany, in the state of North Rhine-Westphalia. It is next to Düsseldorf.
Wuppertal has about 360,000 inhabitants. It has a university, a zoo and daily opened botanical garden, an operahouse, expansive parks and forests with Germany's largest arboretum.

Education and science
The Bergische Universität Wuppertal is a university located in Wuppertal, founded in 1972 and visited by approximately 20,000 students. The Wuppertal Institut für Klima, Umwelt, Energie develops research, strategies and tools in the field of sustainable development at national and international levels. It collaborates with several universities and institutes in Germany and abroad.
