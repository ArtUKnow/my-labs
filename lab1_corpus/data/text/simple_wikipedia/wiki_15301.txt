Tilapia
.]]
Tilapia are a type of fish that is often farmed for food. They live in warm, fresh water, but some species of tilapia live in brackish water. They are a native species of fish in Africa, North America, South America, India, and Sri Lanka, and they are known to be able to quickly take over from native fish in rivers. This has happened in places like the Endeavour River, in Queensland, Australia, where these fish are thought to have been released into the river from an aquarium.
