Maya Lin
Maya Ying Lin (born October 10, 1959) is a Chinese-American architect and artist. She is most famous for her Vietnam Veterans Memorial in Washington, DC and the Civil Rights Monument in Montgomery, Alabama.
In 1981, a jury chose Lin's design for the Vietnam Veterans Memorial from 1,421 entries. She was 21 years old and still an undergraduate at Yale University.
In 2016, Lin was awarded the Presidential Medal of Freedom by Barack Obama.
