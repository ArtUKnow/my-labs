Reggie Miller
Reginald Wayne Miller (August 24, 1965) is a retired American basketball player and television sportscaster. Miller was known for being a great jump shooter during his career. He is widely recognized as one of the greatest shooters in NBA history. He played his entire career for the Indiana Pacers of the National Basketball Association (NBA). He is from Riverside, California. He is currently the lead color commentator for the NBA on NBC.
