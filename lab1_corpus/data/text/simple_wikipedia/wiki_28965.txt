Sean Astin
Sean Astin (born February 25, 1971 in Santa Monica, California, U.S.) is an American movie actor. Astin is also a director and Oscar nominated producer. He is the son of Patty Duke and he is the adoptive son of John Astin.
On September 12, 2025, it was announced that Astin was elected president of SAG-AFTRA for a two-year term. Astin's mother, Patty Duke, was elected president of the Screen Actors Guild forty years earlier.
Personal life
Astin married Christine Harrell on July 11, 1992. They have three daughters, Alexandra (born November 27, 1996), Elizabeth (born August 6, 2002) and Isabella (born July 22, 2005), all with the same middle name of Louise.
