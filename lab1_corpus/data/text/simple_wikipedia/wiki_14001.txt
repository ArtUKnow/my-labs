Cunt
Cunt is an abusive word that is used against people as an insult. It often used as slang for the vagina or vulva. It can also mean as an acronymic euphemism C U Next Tuesday.
Unlike many words of profanity that came from Anglo-Saxon, this word came from a Latin one (cuneus, meaning a wedge shape). After the Norman invasion of England, it replaced the native word for vagina, 'mægeþblæd'.
