List of football clubs in Germany
These are the German football teams that now (2025-2026) playing in a professional league. The highest German league is the Bundesliga (First Division). Below it is the second Bundesliga (Second Division). The Third Division is called 3. Liga. The Fourth Division, called Regionalliga is split into a northern, a southern and a western part. The northern part is called Regionalliga Nord, the southern part Regionalliga Süd and the western part Regionalliga West.
