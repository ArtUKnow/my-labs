Tony Randall
Anthony Leonard Randall (born Aryeh Leonard Rosenberg; February 26, 1920 – May 17, 2004) was an American actor. He was best known for his role as Felix Unger on the television series The Odd Couple. He was born in Tulsa, Oklahoma. In 1975, he won a Primetime Emmy Award. He was nominated for six Golden Globe Awards.
Randall died at a hospital in New York City from pneumonia caused by heart surgery on May 17, 2004 at the age of 84.
