Pelé
Edson Arantes do Nascimento, more commonly known as Pelé, (23 October 1940 – 29 December 2022) was a Brazilian football player. He is among the greatest scorers in history, with 604 league goals, a world record that still stands. In total, Pelé scored 778 goals in 851 games, excluding unofficial friendlies and tour games (career totals of 1324 goals in 1413 games). Widely regarded as the greatest player in the history of the sport, he is listed in the Guinness World Records for most career goals scored in the history of football. He was given the title "Football Player of the Century" by FIFA.
Teams
From 1956 to 1974, Pelé played for Santos, had 1116 appearances, and scored 1091 goals. From 1975 to 1977, he played for New York Cosmos, when he had 64 appearances, and scored 37 goals in official games.

Health and death
In September 2021, Pelé had surgery to remove a tumour on the right side of his colon. He began chemotherapy treatment a few weeks later.
Pelé died on 29 December 2022, at 3:27 pm, at the age of 82, due to multiple organ failure, a complication of colon cancer at a hospital in São Paulo, Brazil.
