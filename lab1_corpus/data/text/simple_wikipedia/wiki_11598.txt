Paul Harvey
Paul Harvey Aurandt (September 4, 1918 – February 28, 2009) was an American radio host.
Life
Aurandt was best known for his short radio program The Rest of the Story, which was broadcast on many radio stations in the United States. He worked in the radio industry for 66 years from 1933 to 2009. He also had news programs twice a day, when he commented on the headlines and told some odd news stories.
Death
Harvey died on February 28, 2009 at a hospital in Phoenix, Arizona. His son Paul Harvey Jr. said, "millions have lost a friend."
