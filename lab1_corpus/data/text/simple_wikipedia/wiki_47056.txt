Sunflowers Interactive Entertainment Software
Sunflowers Interactive Entertainment Software was a German company that developed and published video games. It was located in Heusenstamm, Germany. It was founded in 1993 by Adi Boiko and Wilhelm Hamrozi. Boiko was president of the company and Hamrozi was chief executive officer (CEO). The company was known for the Anno series (also by Max Design), Knights of Honor (by Black Sea Studios) and ParaWorld (by SEK). In 2007 the founders sold the company including all trademarks to Ubisoft.
