Gary Graham
Gary Rand Graham (June 6, 1950 – January 22, 2024) was an American actor, musician, and author. He was known for his roles as Detective Matthew Sikes in the television series Alien Nation (1989–1990) and as Soval, the Vulcan ambassador to Earth in Star Trek: Enterprise.
Graham died from cardiac arrest in Spokane, Washington on January 22, 2024 at the age of 73.
