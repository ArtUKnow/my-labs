Silvia Night
Silvia Night is a fictional (not real) character played by Eva Augusta from Iceland. Silvia Night participated in Eurovision Song Contest 2006 in Greece. She is famous for her larger than life and narcissistic persona. She first appeared in the 2005 Icelandic TV series Sjáumst með Silvíu Nótt. An English language series The Sylvia Night Show followed in 2007.
