They Might Be Giants
They Might Be Giants is an alternative rock band that lives in Brooklyn, New York. The two original band members, John Flansburgh and John Linnell, officially formed the band in 1982.
The band has been given two Grammy Awards, one for the children's album Here Come the 123's and the other for the theme song of Malcolm in the Middle.
