Ape
Apes are simians belonging to the primate family Hominoidea. Its members are called hominoids. They are native to West Africa, Central Africa, South Asia and Southeast Asia. Its living members are divided into two families:
Hylobatidae, consisting of siamangs and other gibbons.
Hominidae, consisting of humans and other great apes.
In everyday use, "ape" often refers only to hominoids that are not humans.
One clear difference between monkeys and apes is that monkeys have tails (regardless of size), and apes do not have tails. There are even differences in their height, size, strength, teeth and the way they move their arms. They have a wide degree of freedom at the shoulder joint, which helps them swing by their arms in the trees (brachiation).
The diets of apes are similar to those of other primates. They eat fruits, nuts, seeds, leaves and sometimes meat. They are generally omnivores, though most of their intake is primarily herbivorous.
