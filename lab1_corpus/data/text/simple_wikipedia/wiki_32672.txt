Trevor Hemmings
Trevor James Hemmings CVO (11 June 1935 – 11 October 2021) was a British billionaire businessman. He lived in the Isle of Man.
Hemmings owned several race horses and the holiday company Pontins. He also owned Littlewoods, Blackpool Tower and had a £150 million stake in Scottish and Newcastle.
One of Hemmings' horses, "Hedgehunter", won the 2005 Grand National at Aintree.
Hemmings died on 11 October 2021, at the age of 86.
