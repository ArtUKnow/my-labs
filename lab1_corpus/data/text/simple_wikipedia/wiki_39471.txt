1549
1549 (MDXLIX) was a common year starting on Tuesday in the Julian calendar. In the Kingdom of England, it was known as "The Year of the Many-Headed Monster". There was an unusually high number of rebellions happening in the country.
Events
Kett's Rebellion
Births
Maria Holl (d. 1634), German woman accused of witchcraft
30 July – Ferdinando I de' Medici, Grand Duke of Tuscany (d. 17 February 1609)
Deaths
10 November – Pope Paul III (b. 29 February 1468), 221st Pope of the Roman Catholic Church
