Shiitake
The shiitake mushroom, or just "shiitake"(椎茸) (Lentinus edodes or Lentinula edodes), is an edible mushroom. It is typically grown on a tree, such as shii, kunugi and oak.
The shiitake is also known as the Chinese black mushroom or black forest mushroom. It is also called "black mushroom".
There are two variant names. For high grades of shiitake there is dōnggū (冬茹: どんこ) ("winter mushroom"). Another is huāgū ("flower mushroom," (香信: こうしん) which has a flower-like cracking pattern on the mushroom's upper surface).
