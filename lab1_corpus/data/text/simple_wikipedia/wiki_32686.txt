Apeldoorn
Apeldoorn is a city in Gelderland in the Netherlands. It has about 160,000 inhabitants. The Het Loo Palace is in Apeldoorn. This was summer home of the Dutch Royal family.
Notable people from Apeldoorn
• Pieter van Vollenhoven (Dutch royal)
• Volkert van der Graaf (murderer of politican Pim Fortuyn in 2002)
• Karst Tates (attacker queensday Apeldoorn 2009)
