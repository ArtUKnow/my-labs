Otherkin is a subculture of people who identify as a non-human thing. Some otherkin believe their identity comes from a non-human spirit, ancestry, or symbolism. People also believe that Otherkin get their identity from neurodivergence.
The term has since been widespread, and it usually refers to many non-human identities, including therians.
Otherkin originally came from Online Communities in the 1990s when people started identifying as elves or other fantasy creatures.
