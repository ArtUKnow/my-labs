Anastas Mikoyan
Anastas Ivanovich Mikoyan (, Anastas Hovhannesi Mikoyan; ; – 21 October 1978) was an Armenian Soviet statesman, diplomat, and Bolshevik revolutionary who remained in power from Lenin, through the eras of Stalin and Khrushchev, to his retirement under Brezhnev. His longevity inspired the popular Russian saying "from Ilyich [Lenin] to Ilyich [Brezhnev] without heart attack and paralysis". As a long-serving Politburo member, Mikoyan was known for his roles in the Soviet food industry, de-Stalinization, and international diplomacy, especially during the Cuban Missile Crisis.
