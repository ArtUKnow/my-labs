Square kilometre
A square kilometer (British English spelling square kilometre, in short km²) is a unit of measurement of area.
It is based on the SI unit meter.
Equal areas
A square that has a side length of 1 kilometer (1000 meters) has an area of one square kilometer.
1,000 m × 1,000 m = 1 km × 1 km = 1,000,000 m² = 1 km²
