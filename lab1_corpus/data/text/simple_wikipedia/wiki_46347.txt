For a Few Dollars More
For a Few Dollars More (1965) is a movie directed by Sergio Leone. It is considered a Spaghetti Western film. Released in Europe in 1965, and released in the United States in 1967, For a Few Dollars More stars Clint Eastwood, Lee Van Cleef, and Gian Maria Volonte.
In the film, Eastwood and Van Cleef's characters are rival bounty hunters who join forces against psychotic bandit El Indio (Volonte) and his gang of miscreants.
For a Few Dollars More is a film in the The Man With No Name trilogy. The first movie in the trilogy is A Fistful of Dollars. For a Few Dollars More is the second film in the trilogy. The third film in the trilogy is The Good, the Bad and the Ugly.
