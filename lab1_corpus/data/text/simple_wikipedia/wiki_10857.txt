Lou Albano
Louis Vincent Albano (July 29, 1933 – October 14, 2009 ) was an Italian-American professional wrestler, Professional wrestling manager, and actor. Albano is best known as Captain Lou Albano. He was a professional wrestler from 1953 to 1969, before becoming a professional wrestling manager in 1996. He also appeared in Cyndi Lauper's music video "Girls Just Want to Have Fun" and played Mario on The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!, based on the series of video games.
