Don Bradman
Sir Donald George Bradman (27 August 1908 – 25 February 2001) was an Australian cricketer. Often called "The Don", he is widely acknowledged as one of the greatest batters of all time. He scored 6,996 runs in his Test career at a world record batting average of 99.94 runs per completed innings.
Playing career
Bradman made his first-class debut in the 1927–28 season, playing for the News South Wales state team. He made his Test debut in November 1928, playing for Australia against England at the The Gabba. His Test career continued until August 1948, when he made his final appearance at The Oval.
Personal life
Bradman married his childhood sweetheart Jessie Martha Menzies on 30 April 1932, and they were together for 65 years until Jessie died in 1997. Their second child (John, born in 1939) caught poliomyelitis, a serious viral disease, and their third child (Shirley, born 1941) had multiple sclerosis.
Death
Bradman died of pneumonia on 25 February 2001, aged 92, at his home in Kensington Park, Adelaide.
