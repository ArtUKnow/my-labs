List of Nobel Prize winners in Literature
The Nobel Prize in Literature is one of many Nobel Prizes given in honor of Alfred Nobel. Every year, a writer is chosen by the Swedish Academy to receive the Nobel Prize in Literature. But in some years, no writer was chosen. The Academy only chooses someone who they think has written something that has great value according to the request of Alfred Nobel.
Since 1901, there have been 122 writers who were given the Nobel Prize in Literature. Eighteen of these winners are women. Only the writer Jean-Paul Sartre willingly rejected it.
