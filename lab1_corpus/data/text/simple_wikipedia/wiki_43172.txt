Raven-Symoné
Raven-Symoné Christina Pearman (born December 10, 1986) is an American actress, model, comedian, and singer. She was born in Atlanta, Georgia. She moved to Ossining, New York at age three. She played the role of "Olivia" in the last three seasons of The Cosby Show (1984) and Nicole Lee on Hangin' with Mr. Cooper (1992).
Pearman is most well known as the title character Raven in the TV show That's So Raven and Galleria in The Cheetah Girls and The Cheetah Girls 2. Pearman goes by the name Raven for most of her acting. She is also the host of the American game show Scrabble.
In August 2013 Raven-Symoné commented on legalizing gay marriage, "I was excited to hear today that more states legalized gay marriage. I, however am not currently getting married, but it is great to know I can now, should I wish to". She was dating AzMarie Livingston since 2012, and in October 2015, she announced that she had broken up with her.
