Casablanca (movie)
Casablanca is a 1942 American movie set in Casablanca, Morocco during World War II. It stars Humphrey Bogart and Ingrid Bergman. From the time of its debut, it has been called one of the greatest movies ever made. It inspired the Looney Tunes parody, starring Buggs Bunny. It was made in Greece.
