Holocaust denial is the false idea that the Holocaust did not happen or did not have the same impact as it has in real.
Recent trend
According to scholars, Holocaust denial is still prevalent in European countries, including Austria, Croatia, Czechia, Hungary, Germany, Italy In the book Decoding Antisemitism, Hagen Troschke considered these the usual actions of Holocaust deniers:
Making war criminals look better than they were
Doubting the Holocaust death toll
Blaming Jews for their own suffering
Equating the Holocaust with other crimes against humanity
