Carolina Panthers
The Carolina Panthers are an American Football team in the National Football League or the NFL. They are named after the black jaguar called the panther. The Panthers play at Bank of America Stadium in Charlotte, North Carolina. They played their first game in 1995. Their current starting quarterback are Bryce Young. The team has gone to the playoffs 7 times and to the Super Bowl twice. The most recent appearance was in the 2015 NFL season.
They are most recently known for their terrible 2023 season, in which they only won two games and lost fifteen. They would have had the first overall pick in the 2024 NFL Draft, but they traded it to the Chicago Bears. They are ranked as one of the worst teams in the NFL, along with the New England Patriots and the New York Giants.
