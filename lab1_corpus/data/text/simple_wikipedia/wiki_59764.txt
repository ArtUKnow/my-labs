Gene Simmons
Gene Simmons (born August 25, 1949 in Haifa, Israel) is an American musician, who is the bassist and one of the singers for the hard rock band Kiss.
Early life
Born with the name of Chaim Witz in Haifa, Israel, Gene emigrated at age 8 with his family to Queens, New York. His mother, Flora Klein, was born in Jand, Hungary. His father, Feri Witz, was also born in Hungary. He adopted the stage name of Gene Simmons in the late 1960s. He lives with his longtime partner Shannon Tweed and has two children, Nick and Sophie.
Kiss career
in 1972 Gene Simmons together with guitarist Paul Stanley formed the band Kiss. Gene and Paul join with bandmates and musicians Ace Frehley on guitar and Peter Criss on drums and vocals. One of the characteristics of the band was their outlandish makeup and costumes. In 1973 Paul Stanley, guitarist and vocialist; Gene Simmons bass and voice; Ace Frehley on lead guitar, released their first album “Kiss.” Gene composed the hit "Strutter" with Stanley and "Deuce,” two of the biggest hits of the band. Kiss recorded their second album, “Hotter Than Hell,”released on October 22, 1974. In 1975 the band released their third album “Dressed to Kill,” with the theme “Rock and Roll All Nite,” the most successful of the band, written by Simmons and Stanley. Gene recorded two of the best live albums "Alive!" (1975) and "Alive II" (1977). In 1976 and 1977 he edited two of the best albums of the band "Destroyer" released on March 15, 1976, and "Love Gun" released on June 30, 1977. Gene Simmons released the solo album "Gene Simmons" in 1978. In 1979 he recorded with Kiss "Dynasty" a disc-themed disco. After the album Unmasked (1980), Peter Criss left the band. Gene and Paul chose Eric Carr to replace Criss, the drummer. In 1982, guitarist Vinnie Vincent was substituted for Ace Frehley. With the newly formed launch the album "Creatures of the Night", Gene Simmons composed the theme "I Love It Loud" one of the most powerful of Kiss. In 1983 Kiss appears without makeup on the cover of the album Lick It Up. The band had changed their style of Hard Rock for the Heavy Metal style. In 1984 they released the album “Animalize,” another powerful album of Heavy Metal. Mark St. John on lead guitar, replaced Vinnie Vincent. Mark left the band in 1985 and was replaced by Bruce Kulick, who would maintain playing lead guitar for KISS until 1997. In 1989 Gene recorded with Kiss one of their best albums, “Hot in the Shade,” with two of the best tracks from the band "Hide Your Heart" and “Forever.” In 1991 the premature death of Eric Carr forced to Gene and Paul to seek a replacements. They chose a young drummer, Eric Singer, who had worked with Alice Cooper. With Singer on drums, the band released the album "Revenge" on May 19, 1992, an album that recalled the band's early records. They also recorded the third live album “Alive III” (1993).
After inviting former members of Kiss, Criss and Frehley, to record the acoustic album, Gene Simmons and Paul Stanley decided, to incorporate Frehley and Criss to the band. With KISS’s original band members again they put on the face paint and left on a United States/World tour. With the original Kiss, Simmons recorded "Psycho Circus" (1998), a studio album after 18 years.
Gene Simmons currently continues performing with Stanley as the head of Kiss. The current formation of Kiss is: Simmons on Bass and vocals, Paul Stanley on guitar and vocals, Tommy Thayer on lead guitar, and Eric Singer on drums.
Other works
Gene Simmons also turned to acting film credits include Runaway (1984), with Tom Selleck and directed by Michael Crichton, Wanted: Dead or Alive (1986), with Rutger Hauer, Red Surf (1990), with George Clooney, Wish You Were Dead (2002), with Christopher Lloyd. And appearances on television series as Miami Vice (1985), The Hitchhiker (1986), Millennium (1998), Third Watch (2004) and more. Simmons and his family starred in the reality show Gene Simmons: Family Jewels (2006-2010).
