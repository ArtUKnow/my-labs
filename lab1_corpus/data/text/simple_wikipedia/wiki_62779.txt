Terry Rakolta
Terry Rakolta (born September 18, 1943) is an American anti-obscenity activist. She is married to John Rakolta, the former CEO of Walbridge. She led an unsuccessful boycott against the Fox Broadcasting Company sitcom Married... with Children Rakolta lives in Bloomfield Hills, Michigan in 1989.
She later founded Americans for Responsible Television to fight against other shows deemed offensive by the organization, including shows by Phil Donahue and Howard Stern.
