1106
Events
September 28 – Henry I of England defeats his older brother Robert Curthose, duke of Normandy, at the Battle of Tinchebray, and imprisons him in Devizes castle; Edgar Atheling and William Clito are also taken prisoner.
Balaguer, Spain, is captured from the Moors by the count of Urgell.
Bolesław III Wrymouth of Poland begins a war against his brother Zbigniew for control of Poland.
