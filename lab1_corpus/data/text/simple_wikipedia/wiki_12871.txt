Canadian Broadcasting Corporation
The Canadian Broadcasting Corporation (), (branded as CBC), is the Canadian public broadcaster for both radio and television. It broadcasts in English and French. The company was founded on 2 November 1936 in Ottawa, Canada.
On November 2, 1936, CBC-Radio Canada was launched.
