President of Iran
The President of Iran is the head of government of the Islamic Republic of Iran. The president is chosen by direct popular vote. Unlike other countries, in Iran, the President is not the leader of the country. The Supreme Leader is the head of state.
The current President of Iran is Masoud Pezeshkian since 28 July 2024.
