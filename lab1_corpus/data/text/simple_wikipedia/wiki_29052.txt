Toyota Aygo
The Toyota Aygo is a car made by the Japanese company Toyota in the Czech Republic since 2005. It has four seats and either two or four doors. The car has a 1-litre petrol or 1,4-litre diesel engine. The car is similar to the Peugeot 107 and Citroën C1. It entered its second generation in 2014.
Engines
1.0 VVT-i 50 kW (68 hp)
1.4 D4-D 40 kW (54 hp)
