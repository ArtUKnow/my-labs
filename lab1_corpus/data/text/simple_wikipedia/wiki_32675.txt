Bielefeld
Bielefeld (; ) is a city in northwest Germany, in the state of North Rhine-Westphalia.
Bielefeld has about 327,000 people living there. It has a university.
Twin towns – sister cities
Bielefeld is twinned with:
Concarneau, France
Enniskillen, Northern Ireland, United Kingdom
