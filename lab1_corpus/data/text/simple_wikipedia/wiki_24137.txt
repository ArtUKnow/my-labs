Googolplex
A googolplex is the number 10 googol , equivalent to 10 10 100 or 10 Ten duotrigintillion or 10 10,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000 . Like the number googol, googolplex was thought of by Milton Sirotta, the nephew of mathematician Edward Kasner. The company Google named their headquarters, the Googleplex, after the number. A googolplexplex / goggolplexian / goggolduplex / goggolplexsquared is a 1 followed by a Googolplex amount of zeros which is 10 googolplex (10 10 10 100 ).
A goggolplexianth is represented as 10 googolplexian .
