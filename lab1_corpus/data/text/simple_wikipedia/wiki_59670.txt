Neolithic
in the Middle Neolithic]]The Neolithic period is the last part of the Stone Age. The word "Neolithic" means “new Stone Age.” It comes from two words in Greek meaning "new" and "lithic" ("stone"). It started when humans began farming, and ended when they began using copper.
Timeline
The Neolithic period started at different times in different places, since not everyone started farming at the same time. The earliest Neolithic period started around 10,000 BC in the Middle East. Scientists call this the Neolithic Revolution. It was a gradual change from nomadic hunting and gathering communities to agriculture and settlement. These eight Neolithic founder crops were emmer wheat, einkorn wheat, hulled barley, peas, lentils, bitter vetch, chickpeas, and flax. They buried their dead in tombs and left jewelry and pottery inside, suggesting that they had a concept of the afterlife.
