Game Boy Color
The Game Boy Color, commonly abbreviated as GBC and sometimes CGB, is a handheld game console made by Nintendo. It was first released in Japan on October 21, 1998.
Sales and discontinuation
The Game Boy and Game Boy Color together sold 118.69 million units worldwide, making it one of the best-selling handheld systems of all time. Its best-selling game was Pokémon Gold and Silver, which sold 23 million units worldwide.
In March 2003, Nintendo sold some of the last units, shortly after the Game Boy Advance SP was released. Nintendo continued to support the console with new software even after the release of the Game Boy Advance in 2001. The last official release for the system was Doraemon no Study Boy: Kanji Yomikaki Master, released exclusively in Japan on July 18, 2003. The last game released in English-speaking markets was Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets, released in North America on November 15, 2002.
