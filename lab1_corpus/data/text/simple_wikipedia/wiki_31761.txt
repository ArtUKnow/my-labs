National song
A National song is a song that represents the culture, history, ideals, or patriotism of a nation. It is often officially recognized by a country and is sung on important national occasions.
A national song is different from a national anthem:
National anthem: The official patriotic song of a country, played at official ceremonies, sports events, and state functions.
National song: A patriotic song that holds special national significance but may not be the official anthem.
For example, in India:
Jana Gana Mana is the National anthem.
Vande Mataram is the National Song.
In simple terms, a national song is a patriotic song that symbolizes a nation's identity and inspires pride among its citizens.
