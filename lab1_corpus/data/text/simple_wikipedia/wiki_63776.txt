Speed (band)
SPEED is a Japanese music group that is has four female singers from Okinawa, Japan. The members are Hiroko Shimabukuro, Eriko Imai, Takako Uehara and Hitoe Arakaki. Hiroko and Eriko are lead vocal singers.
When the group debuted, Hiroko was 12 years old. It made its debut single, “Body & Soul”, in 1996. The group has many songs, which sold million records.
It broke up on March 31, 2000. From 2000 to 2008, it broke up and came back together several times. The group made its latest comeback in 2008.
