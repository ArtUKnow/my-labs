Artavazd Peleshyan
Artavazd Peleshyan (born November 22, 1938) is an Armenian movie director. He makes documentaries.
Peleshyan is also the author of many books, such as his 1988 book Moyo kino (My Cinema).
Peleshyan's work was not widely recognized by world cinema until after the fall of the Soviet Union, after which he made two more short films, Life (1993) and The End (1994).
He is a resident of Moscow, Russia.
