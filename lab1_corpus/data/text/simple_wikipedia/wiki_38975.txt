Neighbours
Neighbours (also known as Australian Neighbors in the United States) is an Australian soap opera. The show ran since March 18, 1985 on the Seven Network, but they cancelled it the same year. Rival television network Network Ten decided to start making and broadcasting the show, and have done that since January 20, 1986. It was produced by Reg Watson. The show started with low ratings, and Network Ten did their best to get more people to watch. They got new characters, two of them were played by Kylie Minogue and Jason Donovan. These two characters became a couple known as "Scott and Charlene", and by the time they married in 1987, ratings were much higher.
The show is about the lives of people that live in a fictional street called Ramsay Street. Ramsay Street is in a fictional suburb, Erinsborough, in the real-life city Melbourne.
