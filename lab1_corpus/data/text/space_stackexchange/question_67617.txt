What potential challenges could make a human Mars landing in 2028 virtually impossible?
In September 2024 Elon Musk stated
The first Starships to Mars will launch in 2 years when the next Earth-Mars transfer window opens. These will be uncrewed to test the reliability of landing intact on Mars. If those landings go well, then the first crewed flights to Mars will be in 4 years.
This topic has a lot of potential for polarized opinion, due to the combination of both remarkable achievements as well as wildly optimistic past statements of the person whose Mars related statement we are evaluating, as well as the complexity and enormity of what Musk says they will accomplish in 2028. And also due to the inherent vagueness in the word impossible, which like the words all and everyone, can either be taken absolutely literally, or to varying degrees of precision based on the context. For example impossible can mean that it goes against the known laws of physics, or it can mean that it goes against known experience and reasonable expectation in the pace and logistics of technical development. I am using the latter definition in my question, thus the qualifying word “virtually”, which itself requires definition, but we don’t need to go that far because I am not looking for definitive proof of impossibility, but rather to identify if there are aspects of the project that are so challenging that in short order they could quickly turn into a roadblock that makes 2028 human Mars landings virtually impossible (Musk implied multiple crewed landings in 2028 in his statement).
While some have dismissed a 2028 goal as “Elon Time”, the fact remains that the CEO and Chief Technology Officer of the world’s leading space launch provider has stated that if everything goes according to plan, humans will land on Mars in 2028. We are evaluating what if any logistical challenges could make that statement either believable or unbelievable. And yes belief is another word that is subject to definition, but I am only asking for reasons for why it might be impossible and some explanation why. A previous question addressed the specific topic about what would need to happen in the planned 2026 uncrewed Mars landing test flights in order to enable a 2028 human landing. Further elaborations on that topic I suppose could either be answered there, or in this question if they are part of a larger aspect of a potential challenge for the planned 2028 crewed missions.
Answers do not need to list all possible areas of challenge, in fact I prefer that they don’t because such a list would likely be too high level with nothing but opinion to support each line item. Better would be answers that specify a few or even just one area of challenge, with some comparison of how fast space technology has moved historically in similar areas, both for NASA and other space development organizations, including SpaceX itself, or technical and industrial development in general. Thus multiple answers addressing different areas of challenge would possibly provide a more complete picture. And would also help answer a larger related question about just how close (or far) we are from human Mars exploration if 2028 is unrealistic.
Risk to human life can be included in an answer, i.e. identifying aspects of Elon’s goal that would likely put the crew in an extremely high risk situation, for example demonstrating a needed technology for the first time on the crewed flights if that is required by the logistics of Elon’s stated 2028 timeline for crewed missions.
