Why "quiet cruise" of a spacecraft would cost NASA 30 mln $ a year?
The 2022 Planetary Decadal Survey (Chapter 22) states that "quiet cruise" phase of an interplanetary spacecraft will cost about 30 million dollars a year for NASA. "Quiet cruise" means flight between launch an arrival to destination, with limited communication (about once in a couple of weeks) an no scientific activities.
https://nap.nationalacademies.org/catalog/26522/origins-worlds-and-life-a-decadal-strategy-for-planetary-science
Examination of recent Discovery and NF cruise costs indicates that a representative quiet cruise phase costs approximately $30 million per year.
