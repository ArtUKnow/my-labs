Accuracy of MRO ephemeris
I am trying to understand if I pick a point on the Martian surface, say the Airy-0 Crater, with what accuracy can I predict the earth time the MRO will pass directly overhead?
It's my understanding that the ephemeris of the MRO is calculated twice weekly using radio astronomy to within an error of a few meters.
I also that the ephemeris of Mars can be measured via VLBI, short term 90 to 150 days, to an error of approximately 50m.
It seems to me that the Ephemerides is now precise enough that the time the MRO passes overhead of a certain point can be predicted with a small amount of error.
