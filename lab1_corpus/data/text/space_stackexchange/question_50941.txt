Why didn't Ingenuity (helicopter) deploy immediately right after rover landing?
Part of NASA's Mars 2020 mission, Ingenuity is planned for deployment in April 2021, about 60 days
after landing with the rover Perseverance in the crater Jezero at Octavia E. Butler Landing on
18 February 2021.
