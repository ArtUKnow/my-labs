Star like object in sky over Maracaibo, Venezuela at 12:10 PM 2/16/2025
I observed a star like object at 12:10 PM on Sunday Feb 16th 2025 80o due north. The object was stationary for 2 minutes and then moved WNW at a velocity I estimate slightly faster that a commercial jet aircraft. The object luminosity diminished as it moved and was observable for approximately 90 seconds after it started moving.
The object appeared as a “very bright star” in the middle of the sky at mid-day. The sun was aprox. 110o due South. I stared at the object for aprox 2 minutes and object did not move. Object then moved on a WNW direction fast.
I assumed this object was reflecting sunlight. The object did not have the shape of an aircraft; it left no contrails. I checked two flight radar apps and there were no aircraft in the vicinity. I assumed object was a spy balloon, but it later moved fast. The object reduced its luminosity as it moved, I assumed due to increasing atmosphere between me and the object as it moved toward the horizon. The object continued to move, it did not stop again or appear stationary.
My questions are: what could the object be? Why did it appear stationary?
