Gemini Spacecraft - External COLOR of Re-entry Control Section and Rendezvous and Recovery Section?
The Crew Cabin section of the Re-entry module of the Gemini spacecraft is constructed of Rene 41 shingles. Although frequently stated as "black" in color, in close-up appearance, they are closer to a gunmetal color which is the natural color of Rene 41. The Re-entry Control Section and Rendezvous and Recovery Section are constructed of beryllium which is naturally a steel-gray color. These two sections appear to be painted black, or a dark gray in color.
Some say they are the same gunmetal color as the Crew Cabin. Does anyone know for certain what the color of the RCS and R&R sections are?
