What is the point of having both ARGOS and the nbl?
ARGOS stands for Active Response Gravity Offload System. It is a specialized robot that can offload all your weight or a certain portion of it to simulate weightlessness or lunar or Martian gravity out of water. What is the point of keeping the NBL with Argos given that water drag is a major issue with that method of spacewalk training. And also the replicas can be higher fidelity with ARGOS due to lack of need to be comparable with an underwater environment. Does the NBL excel as a training method for microgravity spacewalks in earth orbit while ARGOS would be better for partial gravity EVAs on say the moon or mars? Or in the reason that ARGOS can only accommodate one subject at a time? Or is its range too small at least for rehearsing ISS spacewalks?
