How much deeply buried water could realistically exist undiscovered in our solar system?
Although I do not know how widely accepted these findings are, I have recently read several articles which claim that researchers have found evidence to support the existence of an ocean's worth of water underground on Mars. I have also heard it claimed that the majority of the water on Earth exists below the crust.
Assuming that that it is true that very large volumes of water can exist underground whilst remaining undiscovered until recently, this raises the question of how much water could hypothetically exist on other bodies, such as the Moon or Mercury.
How much water could hypothetically exist deep inside large rocky bodies without yet having been noticed?
