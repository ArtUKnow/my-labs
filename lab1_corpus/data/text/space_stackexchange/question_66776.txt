Would it be possible today, in practice, to launch through the eye of a hurricane as portrayed in the 1969 space movie, "Marooned"?
I think that "launch" should mean here to launch the kind of orbital class mission that could send supplies to a space station or (in the case of the movie) rescue some stranded astronauts.
It would make an epic photo!
Some things to consider:
The rocket will probably need to be secured within a hurricane-proof building until the eye reaches the launch site.
It will take some time to get the rocket from that building to the pad, fuel it, and launch it.
Let's assume that it will take 10 hours for this eye to pass over the launch site.
