What dollar value does NASA put on astronauts' “Value of Statistical Life”?
Agencies such as the Department of Transport use a concept known as “Value of Statistical Life” (VSL) when making spending decisions about safety measures such as guard rails, highway lighting and air bags. A value, currently about $13M per life, is set by policy. If calculations show that the proposed program reduces mortality for less than the VSL, it gets the green light.
NASA uses a slightly different process for assessing risk mitigation cost, using “probability of loss of crew” (LOC) and “probability of loss of mission” (LOM) which is currently 1:270.
