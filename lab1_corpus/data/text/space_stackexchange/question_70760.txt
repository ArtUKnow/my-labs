How hard would it be to locate the Apollo 11 launch abort subsystem?
After all, someone retrieved the old F-1 engines. That little cone and tower were at the very top of the first moon rocket.
The tower was jettisoned about 30 seconds after 2nd stage ignition, 3:18 after T0. The tower-abort window (mode I) ended and service-mode aborts (mode II) took over.
Since the first stage came down about 390 miles away, the tower probably landed even farther out in deep water.
Has anyone located it? And would its white paint have survived?
