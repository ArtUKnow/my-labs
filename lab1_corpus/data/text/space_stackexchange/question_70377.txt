What are these straps on these common pressure suits?
Several popular space suits designs feature a strap running from the crotch up to the collar.
American Advanced Crew Escape Suit
Soviet/Russian Sokol(-KM)
It's tied to a pulley that hangs off a steel cable. It looks like the purpose is to let the collar/helmet rotate. My best guess is that this is a way to tie the helmet down, so an inflated suit doesn't start pulling it off a wearer's face (and limiting their vision).
