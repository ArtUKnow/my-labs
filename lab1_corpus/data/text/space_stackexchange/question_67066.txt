What’s the exact difference between an error, fault & failure?
When a rocket explodes or something goes wrong, the anomaly is called something. I think a well known example is with SpaceX Starship rockets flights, where they set certain goals for their launch and called the rest "icing on top of the cake“. Therefore when they achieved the goals, SpaceX called them a success. While in the media the first few flights were considered failures, since there were explosions visible.
Of course there are differences between errors, faults & failures. If I write a code and it doesn’t work, then I say the code has an error, not a failure. In rocketry, it is probably important to differentiate the exact difference between them. Even @DavidHammen said in a comment that it is important to distinguish the difference. What are the criteria needed to declare an anomaly as an error, fault or failure?
