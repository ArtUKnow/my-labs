Is it correct that rocket reuse is much more difficult for the upper stage than the lower stage?

Am I correct? Is the sub-orbital lower stage really so much simpler to reuse than the orbital upper stage? Or, am I, due to some combination of poor sources and poor inferences, mistaken?
