Apollo mini-bike? For real?
NASA considered motorcycle style transportation for use on Lunar missions. Apparently, it didn’t make the cut, despite the fact it “got air” in a 1/6-G test run:
Fig. 4: Test of an Apollo Motorbike prototype flying in the former NASA KC-135 aircraft in parabolic curves to reproduce a 1/6 gravity environment in short 20-second bursts.

According to https://www.americaspace.com/2012/04/06/how-nasa-didnt-drive-on-the-moon consideration was also given to bicycles and a two-seater quadricycle. The minibike (pictured above)
was under development in 1969 for use on Apollo 15. It was a backup
method in case the Lunar Roving Vehicle, the LRV colloquially known as
the Moon Buggy, wasn’t ready in time for the mission’s launch. There
was some talk about the mini bikes incorporated into later Apollo
missions as well.
