Impact of closing the library at the Goddard Space Flight Center
The U.S. government is apparently closing the NASA Goddard Space Flight Center Library.
What is the impact on the space community, including but not limited to the contributors here? I understand that the library houses documents from the 20th century, many of them not digitized. Are they of any major importance? I'm not being sarcastic here — most of us have eventually discarded papers and memorabilia that we had found too valuable to throw away earlier in life, only to realize later that we then never even as much as thought of them.
Therefore the question: How terrible is this decision?
