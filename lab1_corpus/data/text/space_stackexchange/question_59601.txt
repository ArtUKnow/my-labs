What size are the typical keep-inside boxes of geostationary telco satellites?
Geo sats need station-keeping and the size of their keep-inside box is a design parameter. A smaller box implies more manoeuvres to stay inside the box. All depending on the various orbit perturbations.
This will be different from sat-to-sat, but I'm interested in the typical size of the z-component (nadir-zenith direction) of the box of telecommunication satellites. Obviously this affects the signal latencies. I'm curious about the variation of these latencies.
From this site, I'm learning that the xy box size is typically 0.15deg (about 110 km). I didn't find anything on the z value.
I also looked at the UCUSA database. I filtered for active communications satellites and calculated abs(apogee-perigee). After excluding 11 outliers (>300km), I got 278 satellites and an average of 28 km (median 18km). I'm unsure if this is a good proxy for what I'm searching for.
