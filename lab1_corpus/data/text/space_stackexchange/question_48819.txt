How are Experienced and Observed Mission Elapsed Times defined? (EMET & OMET)
To the question titled What timezone would be used for interstellar travel? this currently unsourced answer currently says in toto:
I suspect any interstellar travel will have relativistic effects (it's a factor of even interplanetary travel), the only reasonable timezones are EMET and OMET -- Experienced and Observed Mission Elapsed Times which would have the departure date as its epoch. UTC would fall somewhere in the middle of these.
While the term mission elapsed time is in widespread use in spaceflight, a quick check failed to turn up specific definitions for these two terms. So I'd like to ask the following:
Questions:

Are these generally recognized and well defined concepts that can be sourced or for which citations exist, or are we witnessing the birth of new space acronyms?
