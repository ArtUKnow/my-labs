Why aren't off-axis reflector telescopes more popular for space telescopes? Have there ever been any?
It seems that:
The light gathering capabilities would be slightly better without the blocked aperture (clear aperture is a premium for fairing-limited space telescopes)
The diffraction patterns from the support vanes and central obstruction would be eliminated (the smaller the obstruction, the larger the extent of its diffraction pattern)
More versatile mechanical configurations would potentially be enabled
So why aren't off-axis reflector telescopes more popular for space telescopes? Have there ever been any?
