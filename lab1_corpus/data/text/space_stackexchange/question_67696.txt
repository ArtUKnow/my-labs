What caused fins to be omitted after Saturn 5 designs?
This question (Why did Saturn V have fins?) led me to ponder what changed in rocket design after the Saturn V rockets that led to the lack of fins on rockets (at least that I could find) post-Saturn V? I was unable to find other significant examples of rockets that used fins after that time. Although VonBraun indicated that fins were primarily to assist in case of some propulsion failure in his notes in the referenced question, it's unclear to me if his logic was unsound in practice, if control technology became more advanced which negated the need for passive fins for attitude control, or if some other factor(s) contributed to the lack of fins going forward after that point.
(note that I'm discounting the use of "fins" in something like Buran/Space Shuttle/Starship where the "fins" serve aerodynamic purposes during reentry.)
