Are there any plans to carry out more parallax observations using New-Horizons?
In 2020, New Horizons famously carried out observations of Proxima Centauri and Wolf 359 as part of an experiment to observe the parallax difference between Earth and the spacecraft.
Are there any plans to use this method to determine the distance to stars? I imagine, judging from the quality of images from New Horizons, this would be limited to fairly close stars only.
