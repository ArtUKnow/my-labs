Applying basic fluid dynamics math here, the ratio of the nozzle exit aperture to the Combustion chamber throat, is 750 / 16.4 sqIn = 45.73.
So a reasonable estimate for the pressure at the exit of this nozzle will be:
Nozzle Exit psia = (120 / 46.73) ==> 2.57 psia.
Therefore the Pressure Thrust of this configuration will be approximately:
2.57 psia * 750 sqIn ==> 1926 lbF <== exerted uniformly along interior of the nozzle flare, and at the throat to the combustion chamber
The rest of the 3500 lbF of Thrust (about 1600 lbF) would then be Momentum Thrust (from fuel displacement)
