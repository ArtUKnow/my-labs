For fun I created a planet (earth-mass) and moon (0.025 earth-masses) in a binary orbit with a period of about 2.5 months and an eccentricity of 0.4. The rest of the solar system is modelled as our actual solar system and it behaves reasonably.

I think what I'm seeing is: at apoapsis the delta-g approaches zero at every cycle, at periapsis the distance (peak delta-g) alternates between two sinusoids each of which has a period of about 22*2.5, i.e. about 55 months. These two sinusoids are out of phase with each other.
I re-ran the simulation removing all planets except these two and the pattern is still there.
