\label{frac2}
\mathbf{A}_{O/B}=\begin{bmatrix}

where, $q$$=[q_1\; q_2 \;q_3]^T$ represent the Eulerian axis and $q_4$$=\sqrt{1-q^Tq}$ represents the rotation magnitude. The dynamic and kinematics equations of the satellite are:
\begin{align}
&\dot q=\mathbf{P}(q,q_4)\omega_{O/B},\qquad \dot q_4=-\frac{1}{2}q^T\omega_{O/B} \label{equ2}\\ &\dot\omega_{O/B}=\omega_{O/B}^\times\mathbf{A}_{O/B}\omega_{I/O}-\mathbf{A}_{O/B}\dot\omega_{I/O}-\mathbf{J}^{-1}\omega_{I/B}^\times\mathbf{J}(\omega_{I/B}+L)+\mathbf{J}^{-1}T \label{equ3}
\end{align}
where, $\omega_{O/B}$$=[\omega_x\;\omega_y\;\omega_z]^T$ denotes the satellite's angular velocity relative to the OCS, expressed in BSC. Additionally, $\omega_{I/O}$$=[0\; -\omega_0\; 0]^T$ represents the satellite's angular velocity of OSC with respect to Inertial Coordinate System, where $\omega_{0}$ is the angular orbital velocity of the satellite. q_4&-q_3&q_2\\

-q_2&q_1&q_4
