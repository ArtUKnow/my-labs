Does Starship need fins for roll control?
Space capsules are designed with a hemisperical bottom and a conical top, as this configuration is not only self righting during re-entry, but also keeps the unshielded top inside the plasma stream.
It was noticed, on the sucessful Starship 10 flight, that there was some heat effect on the skin surface above the heat shield.
With a conical top, fins would only be needed for pitch control, and could therefor be much smaller.
Essentially, the design would be a capsule elongated on the pitch axis. Would this be possible with future iterations?
