An alternative architecture for a mission to Mars
This image, which is supposed to represent the current architecture of the mission to Mars by NASA, raised a wave of emotions. People find it too long and complicated. But is it really possible to make it fundamentally simpler? I think that the idea of a single-launch Starship that will land on Mars, where it will use ISRU to produce fuel and return to Earth in 2030 is far from the truth.
