What are the chemical constituents in the orange exhaust smoke from the New Glenn barge landings and how toxic is it?
As part of the NG-3 launch on 19/4/26 the first stage successfully landed on a barge displaying an awesome feat of engineering. On video footage of the landing it can been seen that the landing results in a large dense orange cloud/haze produced. I had expected the Methane-Oxygen BE-4 engines to produce a mostly transparent exhaust composed of water and carbon dioxide (with a small proportion of incomplete combustion products that would give a tint to the exhaust as well as condensation as the water vapour cools into a white mist). The exhaust cloud colour and behaviour seems to have some similarities with an exhaust from a hydrazine type fuel.
