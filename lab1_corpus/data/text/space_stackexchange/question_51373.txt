How did Gagarin's flight affect improvements to the Vostok capsule?
Every spaceflight is an opportunity to learn what does not work well and to improve the vehicle for the next flight. Did Yuri Gagarin's first human spaceflight yield any changes to the capsules or equipment used in later Vostok missions?
This is part of 6 questions honoring the 60th anniversary of Vostok 1, the first human spaceflight.
