Did India purchase the Gaganyaan design from Russia?
Gaganyaan is an orbital crew module under development by the Indian Space Research Organisation. Sources indicate a design based on the Russian Soyuz:
As of 2015 the design utilized a Soyuz-shaped reentry capsule with a mass of 3735 kg, a diameter of 3.100 m and height of 2.678 m.
Does this mean that ISRO actually paid Russia for the design (like China did with Shenzhou), or did they merely make their own copy?
The Wikipedia article does acknowledge Russian help with the life support system, thermal system, and astronaut selection and training. However, this question is about the overall spacecraft design.
