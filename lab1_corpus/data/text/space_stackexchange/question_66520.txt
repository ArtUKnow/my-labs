Does a Lunar Cycler make practical sense?
I know that a Mars Cycler has many advantages due to the distance involved, but what about a Lunar Cycler? From what I've seen, the time window seems relatively short, and docking with a cycler consume quite a lot of fuel. Is it really worth it compared to a Low Earth Orbit (LEO) station or a Moon-orbiting one like the Gateway if the goal is sustained exploration of the Moon?
