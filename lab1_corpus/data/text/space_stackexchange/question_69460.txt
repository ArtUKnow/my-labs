What is the fate of Krypton exhaust from Starlink thrusters?
The chemical composition of the atmosphere is stratified by molecular weight. Above 1000 km it is dominated by He and H2 with single digit molecular weights.
Below LEO it is dominated by N2, O2 and Ar with molecular weights in the 30’s.
In between (at LEO altitude) it is dominated by atomic oxygen with an atomic mass of 16.
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atmospheric_chemistry#Atmospheric_composition
The Krypton exhaust from Starlink thrusters has an atomic mass over 80. Will this gas “work its way” from LEO back down into the lower atmosphere ?
