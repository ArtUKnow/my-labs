While comparing launch vehicles with strap on boosters, it appears to me that the majority of Eastern-European/Asian vehicles arrange the boosters radially, but the Western-European/American vehicles are more likely to employ a pair arranged bilaterally. Here are some examples:

I understand that vehicles launched from Baikonur needed all components to fit on trains, thus limiting the diameter of the core segment. Perhaps this created need for more boosters?
Are there other reasons this geographic trend exists? Different launch needs? Design ethos?
Or am I wrong that this trend even exists?
