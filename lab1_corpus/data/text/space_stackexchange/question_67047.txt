When will the Apollo flags disintegrate?
The flags from Apollo 12, 16, and 17 are confirmed to be intact by the LRO (Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter). However, radiation and extreme temperature are slowly disintegrating the flags. So, approximately, when will the Apollo flags disintegrate?
For this purpose, disintegrate means that a significant part of the flag is no longer attached to the main flag and the flag pole.
