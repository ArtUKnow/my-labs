Will the next deorbiting reactor be on a "safe disposal" trajectory, or re-enter as an uncontrolled derelict?
Kosmos 954 was a reconnaissance satellite launched by the Soviet Union
in 1977, powered by a nuclear reactor containing 50Kg of highly
enriched uranium-235
Soviet officials … lost control over the vehicle, and the system which
was intended to propel the spent reactor core into a safe disposal
orbit … failed.
When the satellite reentered the Earth's atmosphere the following
year, it scattered radioactive debris over northern Canada. The
Canadian government billed the Soviet Union for over $6 million under
the terms of the Outer Space Treaty (which obligates states for
damages caused by their space objects). The USSR eventually paid 3
million Canadian dollars in compensation.[4]
