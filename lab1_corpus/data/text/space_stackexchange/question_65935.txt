Can Starship land on the moon horizontally?
Since the landing engines will be halfway up the body anyways, how about putting the landing legs to extend perpendicular and land with nose and main engine bay pointing towards their respective horizon.
Cons:
Structural integrity (but only at moon gravity)
Bigger footprint
Pros:
Greater stability, higher tolerance for slopes
Closer to the ground
Easier to access internals for converting fuel tanks to a "base"
Main engines can be started sooner after liftoff
Easier to offload cargo
Can be covered with regolith for radiation protection
